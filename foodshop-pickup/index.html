外ngoi6賣maai6自zi6取ceoi2：要jiu3等dang2幾gei2耐noi6、幾gei2點dim2嚟lai4攞lo2、拎ling1走zau2

Takeaway Pickup: How Long to Wait, What Time to Collect, To Go

打daa2去heoi3餐caan1廳teng1叫giu3外ngoi6賣maai6自zi6己gei2去heoi3攞lo2，最zeoi3常soeng4用jung6「幾gei2」字zi6問man6問man6題tai4：幾gei2耐noi6、幾gei2點dim2、幾gei2多do1份fan6。學hok6識sik1「幾gei2」加gaa1量loeng6詞ci4嘅ge3用jung6法faat3，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

When phoning in a takeaway order to collect yourself, you'll use 'gei2' questions all the time: how long, what time, how many. Learn 'gei2' plus measure words, with Jyutping.

飲食購物語法

自zi6取ceoi2 —— 自zi6己gei2去heoi3攞lo2

我ngo5想soeng2叫giu3外ngoi6賣maai6，自zi6取ceoi2。
我想点外卖，自己来取。
I'd like to order takeaway for pickup.

幾gei2耐noi6 —— 問man6時si4間gaan3長coeng4短dyun2

要jiu3等dang2幾gei2耐noi6？
要等多久？
How long is the wait?

大daai6概koi3十sap6五ng5分fan1鐘zung1。
大概十五分钟。
About fifteen minutes.

幾gei2點dim2 —— 問man6時si4間gaan3點dim2

你nei5幾gei2點dim2嚟lai4攞lo2？
你几点来拿？
What time will you come to collect it?

幾gei2多do1份fan6 —— 問man6數sou3量loeng6

「幾gei2多do1」後hau6面min6可ho2以ji5加gaa1量loeng6詞ci4：幾gei2多do1份fan6、幾gei2多do1盒hap6、幾gei2多do1個go3。

乾gon1炒caau2牛ngau4河ho2要jiu3幾gei2多do1份fan6？
干炒牛河要几份？
How many portions of beef chow fun?

拎ling1走zau2 —— 帶daai3走zau2

唔m4該goi1要jiu3多do1兩loeng5對deoi3筷faai3子zi2，拎ling1走zau2。
麻烦多给两双筷子，带走。
Two extra pairs of chopsticks please, it's to go.
