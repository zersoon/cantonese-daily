傾king1天tin1氣hei3：換wun6季gwai3、着zoek3衫saam1、早zou2晚maan5涼loeng4

Weather Small Talk: Changing Seasons, What to Wear, Cool Mornings and Evenings

香hoeng1港gong2人jan4見gin3面min6好hou2鍾zung1意ji3傾king1天tin1氣hei3，講gong2吓haa2換wun6季gwai3、着zoek6咩me1衫saam1。幾gei2句geoi3日jat6常soeng4用jung6語jyu5，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Hong Kongers love chatting about the weather: the changing seasons and what to wear. A few everyday phrases, with Jyutping.

天氣日常會話入門

換wun6季gwai3 —— 换wun6季gwai3

換wun6季gwai3好hou2易ji6病beng6，小siu2心sam1啲di1。
换季很容易生病，小心点。
It's easy to get sick when the seasons change, take care.

着zoek3衫saam1 —— 穿cyun1衣ji1服fuk6

今gam1日jat6唔m4知zi1着zoek3咩me1衫saam1好hou2。
今天不知道穿什么衣服好。
I don't know what to wear today.

早zou2晚maan5涼loeng4 —— 早zou2晚maan5凉loeng4

早zou2晚maan5涼loeng4，記gei3得dak1帶daai3件gin6外ngoi6套tou3。
早晚凉，记得带件外套。
It's cool in the mornings and evenings, remember to bring a jacket.

轉zyun2涼loeng4 —— 转zyun2凉loeng4

終zung1於jyu1轉zyun2涼loeng4喇laa3。
终于转凉了。
It's finally getting cooler.

好hou2天tin1 —— 晴cing4天tin1

今gam1日jat6咁gam3好hou2天tin1，出ceot1去heoi3行haang4吓haa5啦laa1。
今天天气这么好，出去走走吧。
It's such a nice day, let's go out for a walk.
