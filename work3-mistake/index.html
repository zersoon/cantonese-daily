做zou6嘢je5出ceot1錯co3：整zing2錯co3、搞gaau2錯co3、認jing6錯co3、下haa6次ci3小siu2心sam1

Making Mistakes at Work: Getting It Wrong, Owning Up

做zou6嘢je5有jau5錯co3唔m4使sai2驚geng1，學hok6識sik1「整zing2錯co3」、「搞gaau2錯co3」、「認jing6錯co3」，再zoi3講gong2句geoi3「下haa6次ci3小siu2心sam1」，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Made a mistake at work? Learn how to say 'got it wrong', 'mixed up' and 'own up' in Cantonese, with Jyutping.

職場俚語網絡

整zing2錯co3 —— 做zou6錯co3

我ngo5整zing2錯co3咗zo2個go3數sou3。
我把数字弄错了。
I got the figures wrong.

搞gaau2錯co3 —— 弄lung6錯co3

係hai6咪mai6搞gaau2錯co3咗zo2？我ngo5冇mou5收sau1到dou2。
是不是搞错了？我没收到。
Is there a mix-up? I didn't get it.

認jing6錯co3 —— 承sing4認jing6錯co3誤ng6

做zou6錯co3就zau6認jing6錯co3，冇mou5人jan4會wui5怪gwaai3你nei5。
做错了就认错，没人会怪你。
If you're wrong, own up. Nobody will blame you.

下haa6次ci3小siu2心sam1 —— 下haa6次ci3留lau4神san4

唔m4緊gan2要jiu3，下haa6次ci3小siu2心sam1啲di1。
没关系，下次小心点。
It's fine, just be more careful next time.

認錯 jing6 co3 认错 admit a mistake
