入jap6廚cyu4必bit1識sik1：煎zin1、炒caau2、煮zyu2、炸zaa3、蒸zing1

Kitchen Essentials: Pan-fry, Stir-fry, Boil, Deep-fry, Steam

廣gwong2東dung1菜coi3講gong2究gau3煮zyu2法faat3，蒸zing1魚jyu4、炒caau2菜coi3、煎zin1蛋daan2各gok3有jau5講gong2法faat3。屋uk1企kei2煮zyu2飯faan6最zeoi3常soeng4用jung6嘅ge3動dung6詞ci4，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3同tung4例lai6句geoi3。

Cantonese cooking is all about technique: steamed fish, stir-fried greens, fried eggs. The most common cooking verbs used at home, with Jyutping and examples.

飲食進階香港

蒸zing1 —— 用jung6水seoi2蒸zing1氣hei3煮zyu2熟suk6

條tiu4魚jyu2蒸zing1八baat3分fan1鐘zung1就zau6得dak1，唔m4好hou2蒸zing1過gwo3龍lung4。
鱼蒸八分钟就好，别蒸过头。
Steam the fish for eight minutes, don't overdo it.

「過gwo3龍lung4」即zik1係hai6做zou6得dak1太taai3過gwo3，蒸zing1魚jyu4、煮zyu2麵min6都dou1會wui5講gong2。

炒caau2 —— 大daai6火fo2快faai3炒caau2

先sin1將zoeng1蒜syun3頭tau4爆baau3香hoeng1，再zoi3落lok6菜coi3心sam1炒caau2。
先把蒜头爆香，再放菜心炒。
Fry the garlic until fragrant, then add the choi sum and stir-fry.

煎zin1 —— 用jung6少siu2油jau4慢maan6慢maan2煎zin1

煎zin1蛋daan2要jiu3細sai3火fo2，唔m4係hai6好hou2易ji6燶nung1。
煎蛋要小火，不然很容易焦。
Fry eggs on low heat, otherwise they burn easily.

炸zaa3 —— 用jung6大daai6量loeng6油jau4炸zaa3

雞gai1翼jik6炸zaa3到dou3金gam1黃wong4色sik1，就zau6可ho2以ji5上soeng5碟dip2。
鸡翅炸到金黄色，就可以装盘。
Deep-fry the chicken wings until golden, then they're ready to plate.

煮zyu2 / 灼coek3 —— 煮zyu2 / 用jung6滾gwan2水seoi2快faai3速cuk1燙tong3熟suk6

水seoi2滾gwan2咗zo2先sin1落lok6麵min6，煮zyu2三saam1分fan1鐘zung1。
水开了才放面，煮三分钟。
Add the noodles once the water boils, cook for three minutes.

灼coek3啲di1生saang1菜coi3，淋lam4啲di1蠔hou4油jau4。
烫一点生菜，淋点蚝油。
Blanch some lettuce and drizzle on oyster sauce.
