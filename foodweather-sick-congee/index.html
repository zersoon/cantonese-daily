轉zyun2季gwai3冷laang5親can1：上soeng5網mong5搵wan2食sik6療liu4、白baak6粥zuk1、涼loeng4茶caa4

Caught a Chill at the Change of Season: Online Food Remedies, Plain Congee and Herbal Tea

一jat1轉zyun2季gwai3就zau6容jung4易ji6冷laang5親can1，好hou2多do1人jan4會wui2上soeng6網mong5睇tai2食sik6療liu4貼tip1士si2。學hok6吓haa2病beng6咗zo2飲jam2粥zuk1、飲jam2涼loeng4茶caa4嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

The change of season brings colds, and many people look up food remedies online. Learn Cantonese for congee and herbal tea when you're ill, with Jyutping.

飲食天氣網絡

冷laang5親can1 —— 着zoek6凉loeng4

早zou2晚maan5溫wan1差caa1大daai6，我ngo5琴kam4日jat6冷laang5親can1咗zo2。
早晚温差大，我昨天着凉了。
Mornings and nights differ a lot, I caught a chill yesterday.

食sik6療liu4 —— 食sik6疗liu4

我ngo5喺hai2網mong5上soeng6睇tai2咗zo2啲di1食sik6療liu4貼tip1士si2，話waa6要jiu3飲jam2多do1啲di1湯tong1。
我在网上看了些食疗小贴士，说要多喝汤。
I read some food-remedy tips online, they say to drink more soup.

白baak6粥zuk1 —— 白baak6粥zuk1

病beng6咗zo2食sik6唔m4落lok6嘢je5，阿aa3媽maa1煲bou1咗zo2白baak6粥zuk1畀bei2我ngo5。
生病了吃不下东西，妈妈给我煮了白粥。
I couldn't eat while ill, so Mum made me plain congee.

涼loeng4茶caa4 —— 凉loeng4茶caa4

喉hau4嚨lung4痛tung3，去heoi3樓lau4下haa6涼loeng4茶caa4舖pou2飲jam2碗wun2涼loeng4茶caa4。
喉咙痛，去楼下凉茶铺喝碗凉茶。
My throat hurts, I'll have a bowl of herbal tea at the shop downstairs.

戒gaai3口hau2 —— 忌gei6口hau2

醫ji1生sang1叫giu3我ngo5戒gaai3口hau2，凍dung3飲jam2同tung4煎zin1炸zaa3嘢je5都dou1唔m4好hou2食sik6。
医生叫我忌口，冷饮和煎炸的东西都别吃。
The doctor told me to watch my diet, no cold drinks or fried food.
