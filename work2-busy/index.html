Racing a Deadline: Rushing, Running Out of Time, Getting It Done

工gung1作zok3堆deoi1埋maai4一jat1齊cai4點dim2講gong2？「趕gon2」、「做zou6唔m4切cit3」、「搞gaau2掂dim6」係hai6香hoeng1港gong2辦baan6公gung1室sat1日jat6日jat6都dou1聽teng1到dou3嘅ge3字zi6，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Work piling up? 'Gon', 'zou m cit' and 'gaau dim' are words you'll hear every day in a Hong Kong office. With Jyutping.

職場進階日常會話

趕gon2 —— 趕gon2工gung1

呢ni1份fan6嘢je5聽ting1日jat6要jiu3交gaau1，今gam1晚maan5要jiu3趕gon2。
这份工作明天要交，今晚要赶。
This is due tomorrow, so I have to rush it tonight.

做zou6唔m4切cit3 —— 來loi4不bat1及kap6做zou6完jyun4

咁gam3多do1嘢je5，我ngo5一jat1個go3人jan4做zou6唔m4切cit3。
这么多工作，我一个人来不及做完。
There's so much work, I can't finish it all by myself in time.

搞gaau2掂dim6 —— 完jyun4成sing4、辦baan6妥to5

放fong3工gung1之zi1前cin4一jat1定ding6搞gaau2掂dim6。
下班之前一定搞定。
I'll definitely get it done before I leave work.

延jin4期kei4 —— 推teoi1遲ci4

可ho2唔m4可ho2以ji5延jin4期kei4兩loeng5日jat6？
可不可以推迟两天？
Could we push it back two days?

加gaa1班baan1 —— 加gaa1班baan1

呢ni1個go3月jyut6日jat6日jat6都dou1要jiu3加gaa1班baan1。
这个月每天都要加班。
I have to work overtime every day this month.
