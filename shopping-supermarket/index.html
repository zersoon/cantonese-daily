At the Supermarket: On Sale, Buy One Get One Free, Plastic Bags, Paying

行haang4超ciu1市si5最zeoi3常soeng4聽teng1到dou3「特dak6價gaa3」「買maai5一jat1送sung3一jat1」，俾bei2錢cin2時si4收sau1銀ngan4員jyun4會wui5問man6你nei5要jiu3唔m4要jiu3膠gaau1袋doi2。超ciu1市si5必bit1識sik1嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

At the supermarket you'll keep hearing 'on sale' and 'buy one get one free', and the cashier will ask if you need a plastic bag. Must-know supermarket Cantonese, with Jyutping.
