On IG and Facebook you'll keep seeing 'zaan hou', 'daa kaat' and 'jit waa'. The words Hong Kongers use for social media, with Jyutping and examples.

讚zaan3好hou2 —— 點dim2讚zaan3

My photo got almost a hundred likes.
