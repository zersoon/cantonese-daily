Getting Around: Stopping the Minibus, Octopus Card, Changing Lines

喺hai2香hoeng1港gong2搭daap3小siu2巴baa1要jiu3叫giu3「有jau5落lok6」，搭daap3的dik1士si2要jiu3講gong2去heoi3邊bin1度dou6。搭daap3港gong2鐵tit3、巴baa1士si2、的dik1士si2最zeoi3實sat6用jung6嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

In Hong Kong you have to call out to get off a minibus and tell the taxi driver where to go. The most useful Cantonese for the MTR, buses and taxis, with Jyutping.

八baat3達daat6通tung1

八baat3達daat6通tung1係hai6香hoeng1港gong2嘅ge3交gaau1通tung1卡kaa1，搭daap3車ce1、買maai5嘢je5都dou1用jung6得dak1。

我ngo5張zoeng1八baat3達daat6通tung1冇mou5錢cin2喇laa3，要jiu3增zang1值zik6。
我的八达通没钱了，要充值。
My Octopus card is out of money, I need to top it up.
