Shift Work in Cantonese: Morning Shift, Night Shift, Handover

要jiu3輪leon4班baan1嘅ge3工gung1作zok3點dim2講gong2？「返faan2早zou2更gaang1」、「返faan2夜je6更gaang1」、「交gaau1更gaang1」、「換wun6更gaang1」，睇tai2下haa6「返faan2」同tung4「更gang3」點dim2樣joeng2搭daap3配pui3，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Working shifts? Learn how 'faan' combines with 'gaang' for morning shift, night shift, handover and swapping shifts. With Jyutping.

職場俚語語法

早zou2更gaang1 —— 朝ziu1早zou2嗰go2更gang3

我ngo5今gam1個go3禮lai5拜baai3返faan1早zou2更gaang1，七cat1點dim2要jiu3到dou3。
我这个星期上早班，七点要到。
I'm on the morning shift this week, I have to be in by seven.

夜je6更gaang1 —— 夜je6晚maan5嗰go2更gang3

返faan1夜je6更gaang1日jat6夜je6顛din1倒dou2。
上夜班日夜颠倒。
Working nights turns your days and nights upside down.

交gaau1更gaang1 —— 交gaau1接zip3班baan1

交gaau1更gaang1嗰go2陣zan6記gei3得dak1講gong2清cing1楚co2。
交班的时候记得说清楚。
Remember to explain everything clearly at handover.

換wun6更gaang1 —— 同tung4人jan4調diu6更gang3

聽ting1日jat6可ho2唔m4可ho2以ji5同tung4你nei5換wun6更gaang1？
明天可不可以跟你换班？
Could I swap shifts with you tomorrow?
