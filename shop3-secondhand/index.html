Buying and Selling Second-Hand: Listing, Meet-Ups, Like New, Scoring a Find

Listing your old things online, meeting at an MTR station to hand over, checking the condition: Cantonese for second-hand deals.

購物網絡日常會話

放fong3售sau6 —— 出ceot1售sau6

我ngo5想soeng2放fong3售sau6部bou6舊gau6相soeng2機gei1。
我想出售这台旧相机。
I want to sell my old camera.

交gaau1收sau1 —— 当dong1面min2交gaau1易jik6

我ngo5哋dei6喺hai2旺wong6角gok3地dei6鐵tit3站zaam6交gaau1收sau1好hou2唔m4好hou2？
我们在旺角地铁站当面交易好不好？
Shall we do the hand-over at Mong Kok MTR station?

新san1淨zeng6 —— 成sing4色sik1新san1

部bou6機gei1好hou2新san1淨zeng6，得dak1一jat1兩loeng5條tiu4花faa1。
这台机器成色很新，只有一两道划痕。
It's in great condition, with only a scratch or two.

執zap1到dou2 —— 捡gim2到dou3便pin4宜ji2

二ji6百baak3蚊man1買maai5到dou2張zoeng1椅ji2，真zan1係hai6執zap1到dou2。
两百块买到这张椅子，真是捡到便宜了。
Got this chair for 200 dollars, what a find.
