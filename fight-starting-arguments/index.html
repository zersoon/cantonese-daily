How Arguments Start: What Did You Say, Are You Kidding Me, None of Your Business, Do You Think I'm Stupid

香hoeng1港gong2人jan4一jat1開hoi1始ci2嗌aai3交gaau1，通tung1常soeng4第dai6一jat1句geoi3就zau6係hai6「你nei5講gong2咩me1呀aa3」、「你nei5有jau5冇mou5搞gaau2錯co3」。學hok6識sik1呢ne1幾gei2句geoi3，聽teng1得dak1明ming4人jan4哋dei6幾gei2時si4開hoi1始ci2火fo2遮ze1眼ngaan5，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

When Hong Kong people start arguing, the first line is often 'What did you say?' or 'Are you kidding me?' Learn these to recognise when tempers are rising, with Jyutping.

鬧人嗌交日常會話香港

你nei5講gong2咩me1呀aa3 —— 你nei5講gong2乜mat1嘢je5呀aa3（質zat1問man6）

你nei5講gong2咩me1呀aa3？再zoi3講gong2多do1次ci3！
你说什么？再说一遍！
What did you say? Say that again!

用jung6法faat3：語jyu5氣hei3重cung5、聲sing1調diu6拉laai1高gou1就zau6係hai6挑tiu1釁jan6；平ping4淡daam6講gong2只zi2係hai6冇mou5聽teng1清cing1楚co2。

你nei5有jau5冇mou5搞gaau2錯co3 —— 你nei5有jau5冇mou5問man6題tai4呀aa3

你nei5有jau5冇mou5搞gaau2錯co3呀aa3？排paai4隊deoi2都dou1打daa2尖zim1！
你搞什么啊？排队还插队！
Are you kidding me? Cutting the queue?

用jung6法faat3：表biu2達daat6好hou2唔m4滿mun5，對deoi3陌mak6生sang1人jan4都dou1會wui5講gong2，但daan6未mei6算syun3粗cou1魯lou5。

關gwaan1你nei5咩me1事si6 —— 唔m4關gwaan1你nei5事si6

我ngo5幾gei2點dim2返faan1屋uk1企kei2，關gwaan1你nei5咩me1事si6？
我几点回家，关你什么事？
What time I get home is none of your business.

用jung6法faat3：好hou2唔m4客haak3氣hei3，對deoi3屋uk1企kei2人jan4講gong2都dou1會wui5令ling6氣hei3氛fan1即zik1刻hak1變bin3差caa1。

你nei5估gu2我ngo5傻so4㗎gaa4 —— 你nei5以ji5為wai4我ngo5蠢ceon2咩me1

咁gam3大daai6個go3人jan4仲zung6呃aak1我ngo5，你nei5估gu2我ngo5傻so4㗎gaa4？
这么大个人还骗我，你以为我傻吗？
You're lying to me at your age? Do you think I'm stupid?

用jung6法faat3：表biu2示si6唔m4信seon3對deoi3方fong1，帶daai3諷fung3刺ci3，朋pang4友jau5之zi1間gaan1亦jik6可ho2以ji5半bun3開hoi1玩waan4笑siu3講gong2。

搞gaau2邊bin1科fo1 —— 搞gaau2咩me1鬼gwai2、搞gaau2乜mat1嘢je5

問man6人jan4做zou6緊gan2乜mat1、點dim2解gaai2咁gam3做zou6，通tung1常soeng4帶daai3唔m4滿mun5或waak6者ze2唔m4明ming4，語jyu5氣hei3比bei2「你nei5有jau5冇mou5搞gaau2錯co3」輕heng1少siu2少siu2。

你nei5喺hai2度dou6搞gaau2邊bin1科fo1呀aa3？成seng4屋uk1都dou1係hai6水seoi2！
你在搞什么鬼？满屋子都是水！
What on earth are you doing? There's water all over the flat!

用jung6法faat3：屋uk1企kei2人jan4、朋pang4友jau5之zi1間gaan1最zeoi3常soeng4用jung6；對deoi3上soeng6司si1或waak6者ze2唔m4熟suk6嘅ge3人jan4講gong2就zau6好hou2唔m4客haak3氣hei3。
