Picking a Birthday Present: Thoughtfulness, Surprises, Practical Gifts, Don't Spend So Much

另ling6一jat1半bun3生saang1日jat6就zau6快faai3到dou3，送sung3咩me1好hou2？學hok6識sik1講gong2「心sam1意ji3」、「驚ging1喜hei2」、「實sat6用jung6」同tung4「唔m4使sai2咁gam3破po3費fai3」，喺hai2香hoeng1港gong2商soeng1場coeng4揀gaan2禮lai5物mat6都dou1啱aam1用jung6，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Your partner's birthday is coming up, so what should you get? Learn 'thoughtfulness', 'surprise', 'practical' and 'you shouldn't have spent so much' for gift hunting in Hong Kong malls. With Jyutping.

It doesn't matter if it's cheap; what counts is the thought.
