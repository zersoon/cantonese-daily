Ordering Iced Drinks on Your Phone in the Heat: Less Ice, More Ice, No Ice

Too hot to go downstairs? Order an iced lemon tea to your door. Learn how to choose ice and sugar levels in Cantonese, with Jyutping.

飲食天氣網絡

凍dung3檸ning2茶caa4 —— 冰bing1柠ning4檬mung1茶caa4

今gam1日jat6三saam1十sap6四sei3度dou6，叫giu3杯bui1凍dung3檸ning2茶caa4先sin1。
今天三十四度，先点一杯冰柠檬茶。
It's 34 degrees today, let me order an iced lemon tea first.

少siu2冰bing1 —— 冰bing1少siu2一jat1点dim2

喺hai2手sau2機gei1揀gaan2咗zo2少siu2冰bing1，費fai3事si6飲jam2完jyun4頭tau4痛tung3。
在手机上选了少冰，免得喝完头疼。
I picked less ice on the app so I won't get a headache.

多do1冰bing1 —— 冰bing1多do1一jat1点dim2

送sung3到dou3嚟lai4冰bing1都dou1溶jung4晒saai3，下haa6次ci3要jiu3揀gaan2多do1冰bing1。
送到的时候冰都化了，下次要选多冰。
The ice had all melted by delivery, next time I'll pick extra ice.

少siu2甜tim4 —— 少siu2糖tong4

熱jit6天tin1飲jam2甜tim4嘢je5好hou2易ji6口hau2渴hot3，記gei3得dak1撳gam6少siu2甜tim4。
热天喝甜的很容易口渴，记得点少糖。
Sweet drinks make you thirsty in hot weather, remember to tap "less sweet".

送sung3上soeng5樓lau2 —— 送sung3到dou3家gaa1门mun4口hau2

外ngoi6面min6咁gam3曬saai3，我ngo5唔m4落lok6去heoi3攞lo2喇laa3，麻maa4煩faan4送sung3上soeng5樓lau2。
外面太晒了，我不下去拿了，麻烦送上楼。
The sun's so strong outside, I won't come down, please bring it up.
