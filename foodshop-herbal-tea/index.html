涼loeng4茶caa4舖pou3：熱jit6氣hei3、廿jaa6四sei3味mei6、龜gwai1苓ling4膏gou1

At the Herbal Tea Shop: 'Heaty', 24-Herb Tea, Turtle Jelly

食sik6完jyun4煎zin1炸zaa3嘢je5覺gok3得dak1熱jit6氣hei3，香hoeng1港gong2人jan4就zau6會wui5去heoi3涼loeng4茶caa4舖pou3飲jam2碗wun2涼loeng4茶caa4。涼loeng4茶caa4舖pou3常soeng4用jung6詞ci4，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

After too much fried food, Hong Kongers feel 'heaty' and head to a herbal tea shop for a bowl. Common herbal-tea-shop words, with Jyutping.

飲食購物香港

熱jit6氣hei3 —— 上soeng6火fo2

琴kam4日jat6食sik6咗zo2好hou2多do1炸zaa3雞gai1，好hou2熱jit6氣hei3。
昨天吃了很多炸鸡，很上火。
I ate a lot of fried chicken yesterday and feel all 'heaty'.

涼loeng4茶caa4 —— 清cing1熱jit6嘅ge3中zung1藥joek6茶caa4

去heoi3涼loeng4茶caa4舖pou3飲jam2碗wun2涼loeng4茶caa4啦laa1。
去凉茶铺喝碗凉茶吧。
Go to the herbal tea shop and have a bowl of herbal tea.

廿jaa6四sei3味mei6 —— 最zeoi3苦fu2嗰go2款fun2涼loeng4茶caa4

廿jaa6四sei3味mei6好hou2苦fu2，飲jam2完jyun4食sik6粒nap1陳can4皮pei4梅mui2。
廿四味很苦，喝完吃颗陈皮梅。
Twenty-four-herb tea is very bitter. Have a dried plum after.

龜gwai1苓ling4膏gou1 —— 黑hak1色sik1涼loeng4茶caa4啫ze1喱lei2

一jat1碗wun2龜gwai1苓ling4膏gou1，要jiu3多do1啲di1糖tong4水seoi2。
一碗龟苓膏，要多点糖水。
A bowl of turtle jelly with extra syrup, please.

拎ling1走zau2 —— 外ngoi6帶daai3

兩loeng5支zi1竹zuk1蔗ze3水seoi2，拎ling1走zau2。
两瓶竹蔗水，外带。
Two bottles of sugarcane drink, to go.
