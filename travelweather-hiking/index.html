Hiking on a Fine Day: Picking a Day, Taking the Minibus, Drinking Water, Heading Down

天tin1氣hei3好hou2就zau6想soeng2去heoi3行haang4山saan1，但daan6夏haa6天tin1要jiu3小siu2心sam1中zung1暑syu2。學hok6識sik1搭daap3小siu2巴baa1入jap6村cyun1、記gei3得dak1補bou2水seoi2同tung4早zou2啲di1落lok6山saan1，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Fine weather makes you want to go hiking, but summer heat can be dangerous. Learn how to take the minibus in, keep drinking water and head down early. With Jyutping.

Saturday will be sunny, let's pick that day to go hiking.

It's so hot, you need to drink water every little while.

He's dizzy, it might be heatstroke. Quick, find some shade to sit down.
