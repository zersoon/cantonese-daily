Serious or Just Playing: Two-Timing, Cheating, Having an Affair

Is it serious or just for fun? And what do you say when your partner cheats? The most common Cantonese about faithfulness in relationships, with Jyutping.

戀愛進階俚語

認jing6真zan1 / 玩waan2玩waan2吓haa5

你nei5對deoi3佢keoi5係hai6認jing6真zan1，定ding6係hai6玩waan2玩waan2吓haa5？
你对他是认真的，还是玩玩而已？
Are you serious about him, or just playing around?

一jat1腳goek3踏daap6兩loeng5船syun4 —— 同tung4時si4同tung4兩loeng5個go3人jan4拍paak3拖to1

佢keoi5一jat1腳goek3踏daap6兩loeng5船syun4，終zung1於jyu1畀bei2人jan4發faat3現jin6。
他脚踏两条船，终于被人发现了。
He was two-timing and finally got caught.

偷tau1食sik6 —— 背bui3住zyu6另ling6一jat1半bun3同tung4第dai6二ji6個go3人jan4親can1密mat6

「偷tau1食sik6」字zi6面min2係hai6偷tau1偷tau1食sik6嘢je5，喺hai2感gam2情cing4入jap6面min6係hai6指zi2不bat1忠zung1，好hou2常soeng4用jung6。

佢keoi5發faat3現jin6男naam4朋pang4友jau5偷tau1食sik6。
她发现男朋友偷吃。
She found out her boyfriend was cheating.

出ceot1軌gwai2 —— 有jau5外ngoi6遇jyu6

多do1數sou3用jung6嚟lai4講gong2已ji5婚fan1嘅ge3人jan4，語jyu5氣hei3比bei2「偷tau1食sik6」正zing3式sik1啲di1。

佢keoi5哋dei6因jan1為wai6老lou5公gung1出ceot1軌gwai2而ji4離lei4婚fan1。
他们因为老公出轨而离婚。
They divorced because the husband had an affair.
