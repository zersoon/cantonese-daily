落lok6大daai6雨jyu5返faan2公gung1司si1：帶daai3遮ze1、濕sap1身san1、水seoi2浸zam3、截zit6唔m4到dou2車ce1

Heading to the Office in Heavy Rain: Umbrella, Soaked, Flooding, Can't Hail a Cab

朝ziu1早zou2一jat1出ceot1門mun4就zau6大daai6雨jyu5，「帶daai3遮ze1」都dou1「濕sap1身san1」，街gaai1上soeng6「水seoi2浸zam3」，的dik1士si2又jau6「截zit6唔m4到dou2」。學hok6識sik1呢ne1幾gei2句geoi3，落lok6雨jyu5天tin1都dou1講gong2得dak1出ceot1。

Heavy rain as you leave home: an umbrella doesn't stop you getting soaked, the streets flood and you can't hail a taxi. Learn these phrases for rainy mornings.

職場交通天氣

帶daai3遮ze1 —— 帶daai3傘saan3

今gam1日jat6落lok6大daai6雨jyu5，出ceot1門mun4記gei3得dak1帶daai3遮ze1。
今天下大雨，出门记得带伞。
It's pouring today, remember your umbrella.

濕sap1身san1 —— 淋lam4濕sap1

雨jyu5太taai3大daai6，行hang4到dou3公gung1司si1都dou1濕sap1晒saai3身san1。
雨太大，走到公司全身都湿了。
The rain was so heavy I was soaked by the time I got to the office.

水seoi2浸zam3 —— 淹jim1水seoi2

條tiu4路lou6水seoi2浸zam3，巴baa1士si2要jiu3兜dau1路lou6。
那条路淹水，巴士要绕路。
The road is flooded, so the bus has to take a detour.

截zit6唔m4到dou2車ce1 —— 叫giu3不bat1到dou3計gai3程cing4車ce1

落lok6雨jyu5天tin1成sing4日jat6截zit6唔m4到dou2車ce1，最zeoi3好hou2早zou2啲di1出ceot1門mun4。
下雨天老是打不到车，最好早点出门。
You can never get a taxi on rainy days, so better leave early.
