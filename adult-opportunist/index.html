感gam2情cing4入jap6面min6嘅ge3「乘sing4虛heoi1而ji4入jap6」：執zap1番faan1劑zai1、趁can3虛heoi1而ji4入jap6、第dai6三saam1者ze2、上soeng5位wai2

Taking Advantage in Love: Picking Up the Pieces, Swooping In, the Third Party, Moving Up

人jan4哋dei6啱ngaam1啱ngaam1失sat1戀lyun2你nei5就zau6出ceot1現jin6？「執zap1番faan1劑zai1」係hai6講gong2趁can3人jan4情cing4緒seoi5低dai1落lok6嗰go2陣zan6追zeoi1求kau4，帶daai3貶bin2義ji6。幾gei2個go3講gong2感gam2情cing4裏leoi5面min6乘sing4虛heoi1而ji4入jap6嘅ge3常soeng4用jung6講gong2法faat3，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Showing up right after someone's breakup? 'Zap faan zai' means making a move while someone is emotionally vulnerable, and it's not a compliment. Common expressions for taking advantage in relationships, with Jyutping.

戀愛俚語進階

呢ne1幾gei2個go3詞ci4多do1數sou3用jung6嚟lai4批pai1評ping4人jan4，講gong2嘅ge3時si4候hau6要jiu3留lau4意ji3語jyu5氣hei3同tung4場coeng4合hap6。

執zap1番faan1劑zai1 —— 趁can3人jan4情cing4緒seoi5低dai1落lok6去heoi3追zeoi1求kau4

指zi2趁can3人jan4啱ngaam1啱ngaam1失sat1戀lyun2、傷soeng1心sam1或waak6者ze2飲jam2醉zeoi3嘅ge3時si4候hau6接zip3近gan6對deoi3方fong1，等dang2佢keoi5「執zap1」返faan2個go3機gei1會wui6。帶daai3貶bin2義ji6，有jau5「趁can3人jan4之zi1危ngai4」嘅ge3意ji3思si1。

佢keoi5啱aam1啱aam1甩lat1拖to1，你nei5就zau6走zau2去heoi3追zeoi1佢keoi5，係hai6咪mai6想soeng2執zap1番faan1劑zai1？
她刚分手你就去追她，是不是想趁虚而入？
She's just broken up and you're already going after her — are you trying to take advantage?

趁can3虛heoi1而ji4入jap6 —— 乘sing4人jan4之zi1危ngai4

意ji3思si1同tung4「執zap1番faan1劑zai1」差caa1唔m4多do1，但daan6比bei2較gaau3書syu1面min2，正zing3式sik1場coeng4合hap6都dou1可ho2以ji5用jung6。

佢keoi5趁can3虛heoi1而ji4入jap6，喺hai2佢keoi5最zeoi3傷soeng1心sam1嗰go2陣zan6出ceot1現jin6。
他趁虚而入，在她最伤心的时候出现。
He swooped in, showing up when she was at her most heartbroken.

第dai6三saam1者ze2 —— 介gaai3入jap6人jan4哋dei6感gam2情cing4嘅ge3人jan4

佢keoi5哋dei6分fan1手sau2係hai6因jan1為wai6有jau5第dai6三saam1者ze2。
他们分手是因为有第三者。
They broke up because someone else got involved.

上soeng5位wai2 —— 由jau4第dai6三saam1者ze2變bin3正zing3式sik1另ling6一jat1半bun3

原jyun4本bun2係hai6講gong2升sing1職zik1，喺hai2感gam2情cing4入jap6面min6係hai6指zi2第dai6三saam1者ze2最zeoi3後hau6成sing4功gung1取ceoi2代doi6原jyun4本bun2嘅ge3另ling6一jat1半bun3。

佢keoi5終zung1於jyu1上soeng5位wai2，做zou6咗zo2正zing3印jan3女neoi5朋pang4友jau5。
她终于上位，成了正牌女朋友。
She finally moved up and became the official girlfriend.
