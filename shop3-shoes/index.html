買maai5鞋haai4：着zoek3唔m4落lok6、夾gaap3腳goek3、波bo1鞋haai4、半bun3碼maa5

Buying Shoes: Won't Fit, Pinching, Trainers, Half Sizes

鞋haai4着zoek3唔m4落lok6、有jau5啲di1夾gaap3腳goek3、想soeng2試si3大daai6半bun3碼maa5？買maai5鞋haai4時si4最zeoi3常soeng4用jung6嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3同tung4例lai6句geoi3。

Shoes won't go on, pinching your toes, want to try half a size up? The Cantonese you need when buying shoes.

購物日常會話入門

着zoek3唔m4落lok6 —— 穿cyun1不bat1进zeon3去heoi3

對deoi3鞋haai4太taai3細sai3，我ngo5着zoek3唔m4落lok6。
这双鞋太小，我穿不进去。
These shoes are too small, I can't get them on.

夾gaap3腳goek3 —— 挤zai1脚goek3

前cin4面min6有jau5啲di1夾gaap3腳goek3。
前面有点挤脚。
They pinch a bit at the front.

波bo1鞋haai4 —— 运wan6动dung6鞋haai4

我ngo5想soeng2買maai5對deoi3波bo1鞋haai4去heoi3跑paau2步bou6。
我想买双运动鞋去跑步。
I want to buy a pair of trainers for running.

半bun3碼maa5 —— 半bun3码maa5

可ho2唔m4可ho2以ji5試si3大daai6半bun3碼maa5？
可不可以试大半码？
Could I try half a size up?
