Office Small Talk: Lunch, the Pantry, Gossip, Holiday Plans

返faan1工gung1唔m4止zi2做zou6嘢je5，同tung4同tung4事si6「食sik6晏aan3」、喺hai2「茶caa4水seoi2間gaan1」吹ceoi1水seoi2都dou1係hai6日jat6常soeng4，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Office life isn't all work: lunch, the pantry, gossip and holiday plans in Cantonese. With Jyutping.

職場俚語進階

食sik6晏aan3 —— 食sik6午ng5飯faan6

一jat1齊cai4落lok6去heoi3食sik6晏aan3？
一起下去吃午饭？
Want to go down for lunch together?

茶caa4水seoi2間gaan1 —— 辦baan6公gung1室sat1嘅ge3小siu2廚cyu4房fong2

佢keoi5哋dei6喺hai2茶caa4水seoi2間gaan1傾king1緊gan2偈gai2。
他们在茶水间聊天。
They're chatting in the pantry.

講gong2是si6非fei1 —— 講gong2人jan4閒haan4話waa2

唔m4好hou2喺hai2背bui3後hau6講gong2人jan4是si6非fei1。
不要在背后说人闲话。
Don't gossip about people behind their backs.

放fong3假gaa3去heoi3邊bin1 —— 假gaa3期kei4去heoi3咩me1地dei6方fong1

你nei5今gam1年nin4放fong3假gaa3去heoi3邊bin1？
你今年放假去哪儿？
Where are you going on holiday this year?
