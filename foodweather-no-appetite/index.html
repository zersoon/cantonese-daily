熱jit6到dou3冇mou5胃wai6口hau2：又jau6熱jit6又jau6攰gui6、食sik6唔m4落lok6

Too Hot to Eat: Learning the 'Both… and…' Pattern

夏haa6天tin1又jau6熱jit6又jau6焗guk6，成seng4日jat6食sik6唔m4落lok6嘢je5。用jung6「又jau6…又jau6…」同tung4「…到dou3…」講gong2吓haa2熱jit6天tin1冇mou5胃wai6口hau2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Summer is hot and stuffy and you can't eat. Use the patterns 'both… and…' and '…to the point that…' to talk about losing your appetite, with Jyutping.

飲食天氣語法

冇mou5胃wai6口hau2 —— 没mut6胃wai6口hau2

熱jit6到dou3冇mou5胃wai6口hau2，晏aan3晝zau3淨zing6係hai6飲jam2咗zo2杯bui1水seoi2。
热得没胃口，中午只喝了一杯水。
It's so hot I have no appetite, I only had a glass of water for lunch.

又jau6…又jau6… —— 又jau6……又jau6……

「又jau6」後hau6面min6可ho2以ji5放fong3兩loeng5個go3形jing4容jung4詞ci4，一jat1齊cai4形jing4容jung4一jat1樣joeng6嘢je5。

今gam1日jat6又jau6熱jit6又jau6攰gui6，乜mat1都dou1唔m4想soeng2食sik6。
今天又热又累，什么都不想吃。
Today I'm hot and tired, I don't want to eat anything.

呢ni1碗wun2涼loeng4麵min6又jau6凍dung3又jau6爽song2，啱ngaam1晒saai3熱jit6天tin1食sik6。
这碗凉面又凉又爽口，最适合热天吃。
This bowl of cold noodles is cool and springy, perfect for hot days.

食sik6唔m4落lok6 —— 吃hek3不bat1下haa6

咁gam3焗guk6，油jau4膩nei6嘢je5我ngo5真zan1係hai6食sik6唔m4落lok6。
这么闷热，油腻的东西我真的吃不下。
It's so stuffy, I really can't eat anything greasy.

清cing1清cing1哋dei2 —— 清cing1淡daam6一jat1点dim2

熱jit6天tin1食sik6啲di1清cing1清cing1哋dei2嘅ge3嘢je5，例lai6如jyu4冬dung1瓜gwaa1湯tong1。
热天吃点清淡的，例如冬瓜汤。
Eat something light in hot weather, like winter melon soup.
