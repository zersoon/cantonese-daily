Shopping at a Hong Kong wet market: asking prices, choosing fresh food and chatting with stall owners, in a few simple Cantonese phrases with Jyutping.

飲食購物日常會話

買maai5餸sung3 —— 買maai5菜coi3、肉juk6、魚jyu4等dang2煮zyu2飯faan6材coi4料liu2

「餸sung3」係hai6送sung3飯faan6嘅ge3菜coi3，「買maai5餸sung3」就zau6係hai6去heoi3買maai5煮zyu2飯faan6用jung6嘅ge3材coi4料liu2。

我ngo5朝ziu1早zou2去heoi3街gaai1市si5買maai5餸sung3，今gam1晚maan5煮zyu2飯faan6。
我早上去菜市场买菜，今晚做饭。
I'm going to the wet market for groceries this morning to cook dinner tonight.

幾gei2錢cin2一jat1斤gan1 —— 問man6價gaa3錢cin4

街gaai1市si5好hou2多do1嘢je5都dou1係hai6按on3「斤gan1」計gai3，一jat1斤gan1大daai6約joek3六luk6百baak3克hak1。

老lou5闆baan2，菜coi3心sam1幾gei2錢cin2一jat1斤gan1？
老板，菜心多少钱一斤？
Boss, how much is choi sum per catty?

二ji6十sap6蚊man1一jat1斤gan1，好hou2新san1鮮sin1㗎gaa3。
二十块一斤，很新鲜的。
Twenty dollars a catty, it's very fresh.

平peng4啲di1啦laa1 —— 講gong2價gaa3

買maai5咁gam3多do1，平peng4啲di1啦laa1。
买这么多，便宜点吧。
I'm buying so much, give me a better price.

送sung3棵fo1蔥cung1俾bei2我ngo5啦laa1。
送我一根葱吧。
Throw in a spring onion for me.

劏tong1 —— 幫bong1手sau2劏tong1魚jyu4

喺hai2魚jyu4檔dong3買maai5魚jyu4，檔dong3主zyu2通tung1常soeng4會wui5幫bong1你nei5劏tong1埋maai4。

Please clean and gut this fish for me, thanks.

夠gau3秤cing3 —— 份fan6量loeng6夠gau3

放fong3心sam1，一jat1定ding6夠gau3秤cing3。

Don't worry, the weight is definitely right.
