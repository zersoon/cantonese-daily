Buying Appliances: Warranty, Delivery and Installation, Energy Saving, Recycling

買maai5雪syut3櫃gwai6、冷laang5氣hei3機gei1要jiu3問man6清cing1楚co2保bou2養joeng5幾gei2耐noi6、包baau1唔m4包baau1安on1裝zong1、慳haan1唔m4慳haan1電din6。買maai5電din6器hei3必bit1學hok6廣gwong2東dung1話waa2。

When buying a fridge or air conditioner, ask how long the warranty is, whether installation is included and how energy-efficient it is.
