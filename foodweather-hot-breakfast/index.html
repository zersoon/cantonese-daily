Chilly Mornings: Ordering Hot Milk Tea and Hot Lemon Water on Your Phone for Pickup

天tin1氣hei3轉zyun2涼loeng4，返faan1工gung1前cin4喺hai2手sau2機gei1預jyu6先sin1落lok6單daan1一jat1杯bui1熱jit6飲jam2，到dou3咗zo2就zau6攞lo2，慳haan1返faan1排paai4隊deoi2時si4間gaan3。附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

As it gets cooler, order a hot drink on your phone before work and just pick it up, skipping the queue. With Jyutping.

Choose pickup and there's no wait, just grab it at the shop.
