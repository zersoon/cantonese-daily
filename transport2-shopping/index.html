Taking the MTR Shopping: Topping Up, Exits, Bags Full of Shopping

Heading to Mong Kok or Causeway Bay on the MTR at the weekend: top up your Octopus and find the right exit. With Jyutping and examples.

My Octopus card is low, I need to top it up.

For Times Square, take Exit E.

The train is really crowded at the weekend.
