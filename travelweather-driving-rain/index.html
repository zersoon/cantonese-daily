落lok6雨jyu5揸zaa1車ce1：路lou6滑waat6、開hoi1水seoi2撥put6、揸zaa1慢maan6啲di1、睇tai2唔m4清cing1

Driving in the Rain: Slippery Roads, Wipers On, Drive Slower, Can't See Clearly

落lok6雨jyu5天tin1揸zaa1車ce1要jiu3特dak6別bit6小siu2心sam1，路lou6滑waat6又jau6睇tai2唔m4清cing1前cin4面min6。學hok6識sik1講gong2開hoi1水seoi2撥put6、揸zaa1慢maan6啲di1同tung4保bou2持ci4車ce1距keoi5，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Driving in the rain needs extra care: roads are slippery and visibility is poor. Learn 'turn on the wipers', 'slow down' and 'keep your distance'. With Jyutping.

交通天氣進階

路lou6滑waat6 —— 路lou6面min2湿sap1滑waat6

啱ngaam1啱ngaam1落lok6完jyun4雨jyu5，路lou6滑waat6，轉zyun3彎waan1要jiu3小siu2心sam1。
刚下完雨，路滑，转弯要小心。
It just rained and the road is slippery, be careful on the turns.

開hoi1水seoi2撥put6 —— 开hoi1雨jyu5刷caat3

雨jyu5愈jyut6落lok6愈jyut6大daai6，快faai3啲di1開hoi1水seoi2撥put6。
雨越下越大，快开雨刷。
The rain is getting heavier, turn on the wipers.

揸zaa1慢maan6啲di1 —— 开hoi1慢maan6点dim2

前cin4面min6好hou2大daai6霧mou6水seoi2，你nei5揸zaa1慢maan6啲di1啦laa1。
前面雾气很重，你开慢点吧。
It's really misty ahead, drive slower.

保bou2持ci4車ce1距keoi5 —— 保bou2持ci4车ce1距keoi5

落lok6雨jyu5要jiu3保bou2持ci4車ce1距keoi5，唔m4好hou2跟gan1得dak1咁gam3貼tip3。
下雨要保持车距，别跟得那么近。
Keep your distance in the rain, don't tailgate.

睇tai2唔m4清cing1 —— 看hon3不bat1清cing1

塊faai3玻bo1璃lei1起hei2晒saai3霧mou6，我ngo5睇tai2唔m4清cing1前cin4面min6。
玻璃全起雾了，我看不清前面。
The windscreen has fogged up, I can't see ahead.
