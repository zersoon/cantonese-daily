揀gaan2生saang1果gwo2：熟suk6唔m4熟suk6、腍nam4唔m4腍nam4、熟suk6過gwo3龍lung4

Picking Fruit: Ripe or Not, Soft or Hard, Overripe

芒mong1果gwo2要jiu3揀gaan2腍nam4啲di1，蘋ping4果gwo2要jiu3揀gaan2爽song2啲di1，香hoeng1蕉ziu1熟suk6過gwo3龍lung4就zau6唔m4好hou2買maai5。揀gaan2生saang1果gwo2時si4日jat6常soeng4講gong2開hoi1嘅ge3形jing4容jung4詞ci4，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Mangoes should be soft, apples crisp, and overripe bananas are best avoided. The everyday words people use when picking fruit, with Jyutping.

飲食購物俚語

熟suk6唔m4熟suk6 —— 熟suk6咗zo2未mei6

呢ni1個go3芒mong1果gwo2熟suk6唔m4熟suk6㗎gaa3？
这个芒果熟不熟？
Is this mango ripe?

腍nam4 —— 軟jyun5身san1

揸zaa1落lok6去heoi3有jau5啲di1腍nam4，今gam1晚maan5食sik6得dak1。
捏下去有点软，今晚可以吃。
It's a bit soft when you squeeze it, so it's ready tonight.

爽song2 —— 脆ceoi3口hau2

我ngo5鍾zung1意ji3啲di1蘋ping4果gwo2爽song2爽song2哋dei2。
我喜欢苹果脆一点。
I like my apples nice and crisp.

熟suk6過gwo3龍lung4 —— 太taai3熟suk6

「過gwo3龍lung4」即zik1係hai6做zou6得dak1太taai3過gwo3，例lai6如jyu4瞓fan3過gwo3龍lung4。

啲di1香hoeng1蕉ziu1熟suk6過gwo3龍lung4，成seng4身san1黑hak1點dim2。
香蕉熟过头了，全身黑点。
The bananas are overripe and covered in black spots.

生saang1 —— 未mei6熟suk6

啲di1木muk6瓜gwaa1仲zung6好hou2生saang1，放fong3多do1兩loeng5日jat6先sin1食sik6。
木瓜还很生，放多两天再吃。
The papaya is still unripe, leave it a couple more days.
