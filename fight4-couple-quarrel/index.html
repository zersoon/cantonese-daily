Couple Quarrels: 'You Don't Care About Me', 'You've Changed', 'I've Had Enough', 'Let's Calm Down'

拍paak3拖to1點dim2都dou1會wui5嗌aai3交gaau1，呢ne1幾gei2句geoi3幾gei1乎fu4每mui5對deoi3情cing4侶leoi5都dou1講gong2過gwo3。學hok6識sik1「你nei5變bin3咗zo2」、「我ngo5受sau6夠gau3」，亦jik6要jiu3識sik1講gong2「冷laang5靜zing6吓haa5」，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Every couple argues, and almost every couple has said these lines. Learn 'you've changed' and 'I've had enough', plus 'let's calm down', with Jyutping.

鬧人嗌交戀愛日常會話

你nei5唔m4關gwaan1心sam1我ngo5 —— 你nei5不bat1关gwaan1心sam1我ngo5

我ngo5病beng6咗zo2你nei5都dou1唔m4問man6，你nei5根gan1本bun2唔m4關gwaan1心sam1我ngo5！
我病了你都不问，你根本不关心我！
I was sick and you didn't even ask. You don't care about me at all!

用jung6法faat3：加gaa1「根gan1本bun2」語jyu5氣hei3更gang3重cung5。

你nei5變bin3咗zo2 —— 你nei5变bin3了liu5

你nei5變bin3咗zo2，以ji5前cin4你nei5唔m4係hai6咁gam3㗎gaa3。
你变了，以前你不是这样的。
You've changed. You weren't like this before.

用jung6法faat3：情cing4侶leoi5嗌aai3交gaau1經ging1典din2句geoi3，通tung1常soeng4帶daai3住zyu6失sat1望mong6。

我ngo5受sau6夠gau3 —— 我ngo5受sau6够gau3了liu5

我ngo5真zan1係hai6受sau6夠gau3喇laa3！
我真的受够了！
I've really had enough!

用jung6法faat3：句geoi3尾mei5通tung1常soeng4加gaa1「喇laa3」，表biu2示si6忍jan2耐noi6到dou3極gik6限haan6。

冷laang5靜zing6吓haa5 —— 冷laang5静zing6一jat1下haa5

我ngo5哋dei6都dou1冷laang5靜zing6吓haa5先sin1，聽ting1日jat6再zoi3傾king1。
我们都先冷静一下，明天再谈。
Let's both calm down first and talk tomorrow.

用jung6法faat3：唔m4想soeng2再zoi3嗌aai3落lok6去heoi3嗰go2陣zan6，可ho2以ji5提tai4議ji5冷laang5靜zing6吓haa5先sin1。
