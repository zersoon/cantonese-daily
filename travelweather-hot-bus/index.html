Waiting for the Bus in the Heat: Covered Stops, Weak Air-Con, Drenched in Sweat

夏haa6天tin1喺hai2街gaai1邊bin1等dang2車ce1真zan1係hai6辛san1苦fu2，上soeng5到dou3車ce1冷laang5氣hei3又jau6唔m4夠gau3凍dung3。學hok6識sik1講gong2有jau5蓋goi3車ce1站zaam6、一jat1身san1汗hon6同tung4冷laang5氣hei3唔m4夠gau3，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Waiting at a roadside stop in summer is tough, and sometimes the bus air-con isn't cold enough. Learn to talk about covered stops, sweat and weak air-con. With Jyutping.

交通天氣進階

有jau5蓋goi3車ce1站zaam6 —— 有jau5顶ding2棚paang4的dik1车ce1站zaam6

呢ni1個go3站zaam6冇mou5蓋goi3，我ngo5哋dei6去heoi3前cin4面min6嗰go2個go3有jau5蓋goi3車ce1站zaam6等dang2啦laa1。
这个站没有顶棚，我们去前面那个有顶棚的车站等吧。
This stop has no shelter, let's wait at the covered one up ahead.

一jat1身san1汗hon6 —— 满mun5身san1是si6汗hon6

等dang2咗zo2十sap6分fan1鐘zung1車ce1，已ji5經ging1一jat1身san1汗hon6。
等了十分钟车，已经满身是汗。
I waited ten minutes for the bus and I'm already covered in sweat.

冷laang5氣hei3唔m4夠gau3凍dung3 —— 空hung1调tiu4不bat1够gau3冷laang5

架gaa3巴baa1士si2冷laang5氣hei3唔m4夠gau3凍dung3，好hou2似ci5冇mou5開hoi1咁gam2。
这辆公交车空调不够冷，好像没开一样。
The bus air-con isn't cold enough, it's like it's not even on.

坐co5上soeng6層cang4 —— 坐zo6上soeng6层cang4

上soeng6層cang4太taai3陽joeng4照ziu3住zyu6，夏haa6天tin1我ngo5唔m4鍾zung1意ji3坐co5上soeng6層cang4。
上层被太阳照着，夏天我不喜欢坐上层。
The sun shines right on the upper deck, so I don't like sitting upstairs in summer.

搵wan2位wai2坐co5 —— 找zaau2座zo6位wai2

車ce1廂soeng1好hou2迫bik1，搵wan2位wai2坐co5都dou1難naan4。
车厢很挤，连找个座位都难。
The bus is packed, it's hard to even find a seat.
