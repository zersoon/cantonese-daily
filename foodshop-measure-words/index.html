買maai5食sik6物mat6嘅ge3量loeng6詞ci4：一jat1盒hap6、一jat1樽zeon1、一jat1包baau1、一jat1打daa1

Measure Words for Food: a Box, a Bottle, a Pack, a Dozen

買maai5雞gai1蛋daan2要jiu3講gong2「一jat1打daa1」，買maai5牛ngau4奶naai5要jiu3講gong2「一jat1樽zeon1」定ding6「一jat1盒hap6」？買maai5食sik6物mat6最zeoi3常soeng4用jung6嘅ge3量loeng6詞ci4，一jat1次ci3過gwo3學hok6晒saai3，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Do you buy eggs by the dozen, and milk by the bottle or the carton? The most useful measure words for buying food, all in one place, with Jyutping.

飲食購物語法

樽zeon1 —— 瓶ping4裝zong1嘢je5

水seoi2、汽hei3水seoi2、豉si6油jau4都dou1用jung6「樽zeon1」。

唔m4該goi1，兩loeng5樽zeon1水seoi2。
麻烦你，两瓶水。
Two bottles of water, please.

盒hap6 —— 紙zi2盒hap6或waak6者ze2膠gaau1盒hap6裝zong1

買maai5一jat1盒hap6牛ngau4奶naai5，一jat1盒hap6士si6多do1啤be1梨lei2。
买一盒牛奶，一盒草莓。
Buy a carton of milk and a box of strawberries.

包baau1 —— 袋doi6裝zong1嘢je5

呢ni1包baau1米mai5五ng5公gung1斤gan1，好hou2重cung5㗎gaa3。
这包米五公斤，很重的。
This bag of rice is five kilos. It's heavy.

打daa1 —— 十sap6二ji6隻zek3

「打daa2」用jung6嚟lai4講gong2一jat1打daa1嘢je5嗰go2陣zan6讀duk6 daa1，唔m4係hai6 daa2。

雞gai1蛋daan2一jat1打daa1幾gei2錢cin2？
鸡蛋一打多少钱？
How much is a dozen eggs?

罐gun3 —— 罐gun3頭tau2裝zong1

買maai5多do1兩loeng5罐gun3午ng5餐caan1肉juk6，屋uk1企kei2冇mou5晒saai3。
多买两罐午餐肉，家里都没了。
Get two more cans of luncheon meat, we've run out at home.
