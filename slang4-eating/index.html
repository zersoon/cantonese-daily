飽baau2定ding6餓ngo6：肚tou5餓ngo6、頂ding2肚tou5、好hou2飽baau2、食sik6唔m4晒saai3

Hungry or Full: Hungry, A Quick Bite, Stuffed, Can't Finish

肚tou5餓ngo6就zau6搵wan2嘢je5頂ding2肚tou5，食sik6到dou3好hou2飽baau2又jau6食sik6唔m4晒saai3。學hok6識sik1講gong2飽baau2餓ngo6嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2口hau2語jyu5，食sik6飯faan6時si4候hau6即zik1刻hak1用jung6得dak1着zoek6。

Grab a snack when you're hungry, then end up too full to finish. Learn the Cantonese for hungry and full.

俚語飲食日常會話

肚tou5餓ngo6 —— 想soeng2食sik6嘢je5

我ngo5好hou2肚tou5餓ngo6，去heoi3食sik6飯faan6啦laa1。
我好饿，去吃饭吧。
I'm starving, let's go eat.

頂ding2肚tou5 —— 食sik6少siu2少siu2嘢je5暫zaam6時si4止zi2餓ngo6

未mei6到dou3晚maan5飯faan6，食sik6個go3包baau1頂ding2吓haa5肚tou5先sin1。
还没到晚饭，先吃个面包垫垫肚子。
Dinner's a while off — I'll have a bun to tide me over.

好hou2飽baau2 —— 食sik6得dak1好hou2夠gau3

好hou2飽baau2呀aa3，食sik6唔m4落lok6甜tim4品ban2喇laa3。
好饱啊，吃不下甜品了。
I'm stuffed, no room for dessert.

食sik6唔m4晒saai3 —— 食sik6唔m4完jyun4

叫giu3得dak1太taai3多do1，實sat6食sik6唔m4晒saai3。
点得太多，肯定吃不完。
We ordered too much — we'll never finish it.

食sik6唔m4晒saai3可ho2以ji5叫giu3店dim3員jyun4「打daa2包baau1」帶daai3走zau2。
