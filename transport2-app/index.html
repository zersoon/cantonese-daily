用jung6手sau2機gei1搭daap3車ce1：查caa4路lou6線sin3、到dou3站zaam6時si4間gaan3、叫giu3車ce1

Using Your Phone to Get Around: Routes, Arrival Times, Booking a Ride

而ji4家gaa1出ceot1門mun4，好hou2多do1人jan4都dou1會wui5用jung6手sau2機gei1查caa4路lou6線sin3、睇tai2巴baa1士si2幾gei2時si4到dou3，或waak6者ze2叫giu3車ce1。呢ne1幾gei2句geoi3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2好hou2實sat6用jung6，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

These days most people use their phone to check routes, see when the bus is coming or book a ride. Handy Cantonese for that, with Jyutping.

交通網絡日常會話

查caa4路lou6線sin3 —— 查caa4路lou6线sin3

我ngo5用jung6手sau2機gei1查caa4吓haa5路lou6線sin3先sin1。
我先用手机查一下路线。
Let me check the route on my phone first.

到dou3站zaam6時si4間gaan3 —— 到dou3站zaam6时si4间gaan3

個go3 app 話waa6巴baa1士si2仲zung6有jau5五ng5分fan1鐘zung1到dou3。
那个 app 说公交车还有五分钟到。
The app says the bus will arrive in five minutes.

叫giu3車ce1 —— 叫giu3车ce1

落lok6雨jyu5好hou2難naan4截zit6的dik1士si2，不bat1如jyu4用jung6手sau2機gei1叫giu3車ce1。
下雨很难拦到出租车，不如用手机叫车。
It's hard to hail a taxi in the rain, let's book one on the phone.

司si1機gei1到dou3咗zo2樓lau4下haa6，快faai3啲di1落lok6嚟lei4。
司机到楼下了，快点下来。
The driver is downstairs, come down quickly.

冇mou5網mong5 —— 没mut6有jau5网mong5络lok6

喺hai2隧seoi6道dou6冇mou5網mong5，睇tai2唔m4到dou2地dei6圖tou4。
在隧道里没网络，看不到地图。
There's no signal in the tunnel, so I can't see the map.
