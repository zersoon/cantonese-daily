Dating Talk: Going Out, Showing Off, Third Wheel, Breaking Up, Single

香hoeng1港gong2人jan4談taam4戀lyun2愛oi3叫giu3「拍paak3拖to1」，跟gan1住zyu6情cing4侶leoi5出ceot1街gaai1叫giu3做zou6「電din6燈dang1膽daam2」。講gong2拍paak3拖to1最zeoi3常soeng4用jung6嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3同tung4由jau4來loi4。

In Hong Kong, dating is 'paak to', and tagging along with a couple makes you a 'light bulb'. The most common Cantonese for dating, with Jyutping and origins.

俚語戀愛香港

拍paak3拖to1 —— 談taam4戀lyun2愛oi3

以ji5前cin4嘅ge3船syun4並bing6排paai4行hang4叫giu3「拍paak3拖to1」，就zau6好hou2似ci5兩loeng5個go3人jan4手sau2拖to1手sau2咁gam3。

佢keoi5哋dei6拍paak3咗zo2三saam1年nin4拖to1。
他们谈了三年恋爱。
They've been dating for three years.

放fong3閃sim2 —— 曬saai3恩jan1愛oi3

情cing4侶leoi5喺hai2人jan4前cin4或waak6者ze2社se5交gaau1網mong5曬saai3甜tim4蜜mat6。

佢keoi5哋dei6又jau6喺hai2 IG 放fong3閃sim2。
他们又在 IG 秀恩爱。
They're showing off their relationship on IG again.

電din6燈dang1膽daam2 —— 電din6燈dang1泡paau1

你nei5兩loeng5個go3去heoi3啦laa1，我ngo5唔m4做zou6電din6燈dang1膽daam2喇laa3。
你们俩去吧，我不当电灯泡了。
You two go ahead, I don't want to be the third wheel.

甩lat1拖to1 —— 分fan1手sau2

佢keoi5琴kam4日jat6同tung4男naam4朋pang4友jau5甩lat1咗zo2拖to1。
她昨天跟男朋友分手了。
She broke up with her boyfriend yesterday.

單daan1身san1 —— 冇mou5拍paak3拖to1

我ngo5單daan1身san1咗zo2兩loeng5年nin4。
我单身了两年。
I've been single for two years.
