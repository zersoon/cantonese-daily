Late for Work: Oversleeping, Traffic Jams, Running Late

返faan1工gung1遲ci4到dou3要jiu3同tung4老lou5細sai3交gaau1代doi6？「瞓fan3過gwo3龍lung4」、「塞sak1車ce1」、「遲ci4咗zo2」幾gei2個go3講gong2法faat3一jat1次ci3學hok6識sik1，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Need to explain why you're late? Learn 'oversleep', 'traffic jam' and 'I'm late' in Cantonese, with Jyutping.

職場俚語網絡

瞓fan3過gwo3龍lung4 —— 睡seoi6過gwo3頭tau4

今gam1朝ziu1瞓fan3過gwo3龍lung4，遲ci4咗zo2半bun3個go3鐘zung1。
今早睡过头，迟到了半个小时。
I overslept this morning and was half an hour late.

塞sak1車ce1 —— 交gaau1通tung1擠zai1塞sak1

隧seoi6道dou6好hou2塞sak1車ce1，我ngo5會wui5遲ci4少siu2少siu2。
隧道很堵，我会晚一点到。
The tunnel is jammed, I'll be a little late.

遲ci4咗zo2 —— 遲ci4到dou3咗zo2

唔m4好hou2意ji3思si1，我ngo5遲ci4咗zo2，而ji4家gaa1到dou3緊gan2。
不好意思，我迟到了，现在快到了。
Sorry, I'm late, I'm on my way now.

趕gon2住zyu6出ceot1門mun4 —— 急gap1急gap1腳goek3出ceot1門mun4

我ngo5趕gon2住zyu6出ceot1門mun4，連lin4早zou2餐caan1都dou1冇mou5食sik6。
我匆忙出门，连早餐都没吃。
I rushed out the door and didn't even have breakfast.
