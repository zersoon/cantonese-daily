網mong5上soeng6訂deng6花faa1：一jat1束cuk1玫mui4瑰gwai3、送sung3到dou3公gung1司si1、寫se2心sam1意ji3卡kaat1

Ordering Flowers Online: A Bouquet of Roses, Delivery to the Office, Writing a Card

情cing4人jan4節zit3想soeng2送sung3花faa1畀bei2另ling6一jat1半bun3？網mong5上soeng6訂deng6花faa1要jiu3揀gaan2花faa1束cuk1、填tin4地dei6址zi2、寫se2心sam1意ji3卡kaat1，仲zung6要jiu3確kok3保bou2準zeon2時si4送sung3到dou3。附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3同tung4例lai6句geoi3。

Want to send flowers on Valentine's Day? Ordering online means choosing a bouquet, filling in the address, writing a card and making sure it arrives on time. With Jyutping.

訂deng6花faa1 —— 订ding3花faa1

情cing4人jan4節zit3啲di1花faa1好hou2貴gwai3，要jiu3預jyu6早zou2訂deng6花faa1。
情人节的花很贵，要提早订花。
Flowers are pricey on Valentine's Day, so book them early.

一jat1束cuk1玫mui4瑰gwai3 —— 一jat1束cuk1玫mui4瑰gwai3

我ngo5喺hai2網mong5上soeng6訂deng6咗zo2一jat1束cuk1玫mui4瑰gwai3。
我在网上订了一束玫瑰。
I ordered a bouquet of roses online.

送sung3到dou3公gung1司si1 —— 送sung3到dou3公gung1司si1

可ho2唔m4可ho2以ji5送sung3到dou3佢keoi5公gung1司si1？佢keoi5一jat1點dim2前cin4要jiu3收sau1到dou2。
可以送到她公司吗？她一点前要收到。
Can it be delivered to her office? She needs it before one o'clock.

心sam1意ji3卡kaat1 —— 贺ho6卡kaat1

心sam1意ji3卡kaat1上soeng6面min6寫se2：情cing4人jan4節zit3快faai3樂lok6。
卡片上写：情人节快乐。
On the card write: Happy Valentine's Day.

準zeon2時si4 —— 准zeon2时si4

啲di1花faa1準zeon2時si4送sung3到dou3，佢keoi5好hou2開hoi1心sam1。
花准时送到，她很开心。
The flowers arrived on time and she was really happy.
