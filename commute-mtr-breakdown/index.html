港gong2鐵tit3壞waai6車ce1點dim2算syun3：停ting4咗zo2、迫bik1到dou3爆baau3、證zing3明ming4信seon3、改goi2搭daap3

When the MTR Breaks Down: Stopped, Packed, Proof Letter, Switching Routes

朝ziu1早zou2港gong2鐵tit3出ceot1事si6，月jyut6台toi4迫bik1到dou3爆baau3，點dim2同tung4公gung1司si1解gaai2釋sik1？學hok6識sik1「壞waai6車ce1」、「改goi2搭daap3」，仲zung6有jau5可ho2以ji5喺hai2網mong5上soeng6攞lo2嘅ge3「證zing3明ming4信seon3」。

The MTR breaks down in the morning and the platform is packed. How do you explain it at work? Learn 'waai ce', 'goi daap' and the 'proof letter' you can get online.

職場交通網絡

壞waai6車ce1 / 停ting4咗zo2 —— 列lit6車ce1故gu3障zoeng3 / 停ting4了liu5

荃cyun4灣waan1綫sin3壞waai6車ce1，列lit6車ce1停ting4咗zo2喺hai2隧seoi6道dou6。
荃湾线故障，列车停在隧道里。
The Tsuen Wan Line broke down and the train stopped in the tunnel.

迫bik1到dou3爆baau3 —— 擠zai1得dak1要jiu3命meng6

月jyut6台toi4迫bik1到dou3爆baau3，我ngo5等dang2咗zo2三saam1班baan1車ce1都dou1上soeng5唔m4到dou2。
站台挤得要命，我等了三班车都上不去。
The platform was packed, I waited for three trains and couldn't get on.

證zing3明ming4信seon3 —— 延jin4誤ng6證zing3明ming4

你nei5可ho2以ji5喺hai2網mong5上soeng6攞lo2證zing3明ming4信seon3，畀bei2人jan4事si6部bou6睇tai2。
你可以在网上拿延误证明，给人事部看。
You can get a proof-of-delay letter online and show it to HR.

改goi2搭daap3 —— 改goi2乘sing4

我ngo5睇tai2手sau2機gei1見gin3到dou2話waa6壞waai6車ce1，即zik1刻hak1改goi2搭daap3巴baa1士si2。
我看手机见到说故障，马上改坐巴士。
I saw the breakdown on my phone and switched to the bus right away.
