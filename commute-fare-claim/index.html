公gung1司si1報bou3車ce1費fai3：車ce1費fai3、收sau1據geoi3、影jing2低dai1、報bou3數sou3

Claiming Travel Costs from Work: Fares, Receipts, Taking a Photo, Submitting a Claim

見gin3客haak3搭daap3的dik1士si2，車ce1費fai3可ho2以ji5問man6公gung1司si1攞lo2返faan2。記gei3得dak1攞lo2「收sau1據geoi3」、用jung6手sau2機gei1「影jing2低dai1」，喺hai2網mong5上soeng6系hai6統tung2「報bou3數sou3」。

Took a taxi to see a client? You can get the fare back from the company. Keep the receipt, take a photo with your phone and submit the claim online.

職場交通網絡

車ce1費fai3 —— 交gaau1通tung1費fai3

去heoi3見gin3客haak3嘅ge3車ce1費fai3，公gung1司si1會wui5出ceot1。
去见客户的交通费，公司会付。
The company pays the fare when you go to meet clients.

收sau1據geoi3 —— 收sau1據geoi3、發faat3票piu3

落lok6車ce1記gei3得dak1問man6司si1機gei1攞lo2收sau1據geoi3。
下车记得向司机要收据。
Remember to ask the driver for a receipt when you get out.

影jing2低dai1 —— 拍paak3下haa6來loi4

張zoeng1收sau1據geoi3好hou2易ji6唔m4見gin3，最zeoi3好hou2用jung6手sau2機gei1影jing2低dai1。
收据很容易丢，最好用手机拍下来。
Receipts get lost easily, so it's best to take a photo with your phone.

報bou3數sou3 —— 報bou3銷siu1

月jyut6尾mei5之zi1前cin4要jiu3喺hai2網mong5上soeng6報bou3數sou3。
月底之前要在网上报销。
You have to submit your claim online before the end of the month.
