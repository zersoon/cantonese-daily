安on1全cyun4同tung4健gin6康hong1：安on1全cyun4套tou3、避bei6孕jan6、做zou6檢gim2查caa4

Safety and Health: Condoms, Contraception, Getting Checked

講gong2到dou3兩loeng5性sing3健gin6康hong1，正zing3規kwai1嘅ge3講gong2法faat3同tung4診can2所so2用jung6語jyu5都dou1要jiu3識sik1。安on1全cyun4套tou3、避bei6孕jan6、做zou6身san1體tai2檢gim2查caa4點dim2講gong2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

When it comes to sexual health, it helps to know the proper terms and clinic language. How to say condoms, contraception and getting a check-up in Cantonese, with Jyutping.

戀愛進階購物

安on1全cyun4套tou3 —— 避bei6孕jan6套tou3

藥joek6房fong4同tung4便bin6利lei6店dim3都dou1有jau5得dak1賣maai6。

唔m4該goi1，安on1全cyun4套tou3喺hai2邊bin1度dou6？
请问，安全套在哪里？
Excuse me, where are the condoms?

避bei6孕jan6

我ngo5哋dei6每mui5次ci3都dou1有jau5做zou6好hou2避bei6孕jan6。
我们每次都做好避孕措施。
We always use contraception.

避bei6孕jan6丸jyun2 —— 避bei6孕jan6藥joek6

食sik6避bei6孕jan6丸jyun2之zi1前cin4，最zeoi3好hou2問man6吓haa5醫ji1生sang1。
吃避孕药之前，最好问问医生。
Before taking the pill, it's best to ask a doctor.

做zou6檢gim2查caa4 —— 身san1體tai2檢gim2查caa4

有jau5咩me1擔daam1心sam1，可ho2以ji5去heoi3診can2所so2做zou6檢gim2查caa4。
有什么担心，可以去诊所做检查。
If you're worried about anything, you can get checked at a clinic.
