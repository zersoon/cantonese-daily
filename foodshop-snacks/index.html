買maai5零ling4食sik6：口hau2痕han4、為wai6食sik6、停ting4唔m4到dou2口hau2

Buying Snacks: Peckish, Greedy, Can't Stop Eating

一jat1入jap6零ling4食sik6舖pou3就zau6忍jan2唔m4住zyu6手sau2？講gong2食sik6零ling4食sik6嘅ge3日jat6常soeng4廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，由jau4「口hau2痕han4」到dou3「食sik6到dou3停ting4唔m4到dou2口hau2」，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Can't help yourself in a snack shop? Everyday Cantonese for snacking, from 'peckish' to 'can't stop eating', with Jyutping.

飲食購物俚語

口hau2痕han4 —— 嘴zeoi2饞caam4，想soeng2食sik6嘢je5

好hou2口hau2痕han4呀aa3，落lok6去heoi3買maai5包baau1薯syu4片pin2先sin1。
嘴好馋啊，先下去买包薯片。
I feel like snacking. Let me go down and buy a bag of crisps.

為wai6食sik6 —— 貪taam1食sik6

你nei5真zan1係hai6為wai6食sik6，一jat1次ci3買maai5咁gam3多do1朱zyu1古gu1力lik1。
你真贪吃，一次买这么多巧克力。
You're such a glutton, buying that much chocolate at once.

停ting4唔m4到dou2口hau2 —— 一jat1路lou6食sik6唔m4停ting4得dak1

呢ni1隻zek3魷jau4魚jyu4絲si1好hou2好hou2食sik6，停ting4唔m4到dou2口hau2。
这种鱿鱼丝很好吃，停不了嘴。
This shredded squid is so good, I can't stop eating it.

掃sou3貨fo3 —— 大daai6手sau2買maai5

零ling4食sik6舖pou3減gaam2價gaa3，佢keoi5即zik1刻haak1去heoi3掃sou3貨fo3。
零食店打折，他马上去大买一通。
The snack shop had a sale, and he went straight there to stock up.
