Yum Cha: Tea and Dim Sum, Har Gow, Siu Mai, More Hot Water

廣gwong2東dung1人jan4講gong2「飲jam2茶caa4」唔m4止zi2飲jam2茶caa4，仲zung6要jiu3食sik6點dim2心sam1。由jau4點dim2心sam1名meng2到dou3點dim2樣joeng2叫giu3伙fo2記gei3加gaa1水seoi2，飲jam2茶caa4必bit1識sik1嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

For Cantonese people, 'drinking tea' means dim sum too. From dumpling names to asking for more hot water, the Cantonese you need for yum cha, with Jyutping.

飲食日常會話入門

一jat1盅zung1兩loeng5件gin6

一jat1壺wu4茶caa4配pui3兩loeng5件gin6點dim2心sam1，係hai6傳cyun4統tung2飲jam2茶caa4嘅ge3講gong2法faat3。

星sing1期kei4日jat6同tung4屋uk1企kei2人jan4去heoi3飲jam2茶caa4，一jat1盅zung1兩loeng5件gin6。
星期天和家人去喝早茶，一壶茶两件点心。
On Sunday I'm going for yum cha with the family — tea and a couple of dim sum.

必bit1食sik6點dim2心sam1

要jiu3一jat1籠lung4蝦haa1餃gaau2，一jat1籠lung4燒siu1賣maai2。
要一笼虾饺，一笼烧卖。
One basket of har gow and one of siu mai, please.

加gaa1水seoi2 —— 添tim1熱jit6水seoi2

茶caa4壺wu2冇mou5水seoi2，可ho2以ji5將zoeng1壺wu4蓋goi3打daa2開hoi1擺baai2側zak1邊bin1，伙fo2記gei3就zau6知zi1要jiu3加gaa1水seoi2。

唔m4該goi1，加gaa1水seoi2。
麻烦加点热水。
More hot water, please.
