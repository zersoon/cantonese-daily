凍dung3天tin1同tung4回wui4南naam4天tin1：好hou2凍dung3、寒hon4流lau4、抽cau1濕sap1機gei1

Cold Snaps and Humid Spells: Freezing, Cold Fronts, Dehumidifiers

香hoeng1港gong2冬dung1天tin1有jau5寒hon4流lau4，春ceon1天tin1有jau5「回wui4南naam4天tin1」，地dei6下haa2濕sap1到dou3好hou2似ci5落lok6咗zo2雨jyu5。講gong2凍dung3天tin1同tung4潮ciu4濕sap1天tin1氣hei3嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Hong Kong winters bring cold fronts, and spring brings 'wui naam tin', when floors get so damp it looks like it rained indoors. Cantonese for cold and humid weather, with Jyutping.

天氣日常會話香港

好hou2凍dung3 —— 好hou2冷laang5

今gam1朝ziu1好hou2凍dung3，記gei3得dak1着zoek3多do1件gin6衫saam1。
今早好冷，记得多穿件衣服。
It's freezing this morning, remember to wear an extra layer.

寒hon4流lau4 —— 冷laang5空hung1氣hei3

聽ting1日jat6有jau5寒hon4流lau4，氣hei3溫wan1會wui5跌dit3到dou3十sap6度dou6。
明天有寒流，气温会降到十度。
A cold front arrives tomorrow; the temperature will drop to ten degrees.

回wui4南naam4天tin1 —— 春ceon1天tin1又jau6濕sap1又jau6焗guk6

牆coeng4身san1、地dei6板baan2都dou1會wui5「出ceot1水seoi2」，衫saam1都dou1晾long6唔m4乾gon1。

回wui4南naam4天tin1啲di1衫saam1成seng4日jat6都dou1唔m4乾gon1。
回南天衣服总是不干。
During the humid spell, the laundry never dries.

抽cau1濕sap1機gei1 —— 除ceoi4濕sap1機gei1

快faai3啲di1開hoi1抽cau1濕sap1機gei1！
快点开除湿机！
Turn on the dehumidifier, quick!
