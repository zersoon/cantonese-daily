用jung6手sau2機gei1叫giu3外ngoi6賣maai6：落lok6單daan1、送sung3餐caan1、到dou3咗zo2樓lau4下haa6

Ordering Food Delivery on Your Phone: Placing Orders, Delivery, 'I'm Downstairs'

懶laan2得dak1出ceot1街gaai1食sik6飯faan6？用jung6手sau2機gei1 app 叫giu3外ngoi6賣maai6好hou2方fong1便bin6。落lok6單daan1、等dang2送sung3餐caan1、同tung4外ngoi6賣maai6員jyun4溝kau1通tung1嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Too lazy to go out? Ordering delivery on an app is easy. The Cantonese for placing orders, waiting, and talking with the rider, with Jyutping.

飲食網絡日常會話

叫giu3外ngoi6賣maai6 —— 點dim2外ngoi6賣maai6

落lok6雨jyu5唔m4想soeng2出ceot1街gaai1，不bat1如jyu4叫giu3外ngoi6賣maai6啦laa1。
下雨不想出门，不如点外卖吧。
It's raining and I don't want to go out, let's order delivery.

落lok6單daan1 —— 下haa6單daan1

我ngo5喺hai2 app 落lok6咗zo2單daan1，話waa6要jiu3等dang2四sei3十sap6分fan1鐘zung1。
我在 app 下了单，说要等四十分钟。
I placed the order on the app, it says a forty-minute wait.

有jau5冇mou5優jau1惠wai6碼maa5？滿mun5一jat1百baak3蚊man1免min5運wan6費fai3。
有没有优惠码？满一百块免运费。
Any promo code? Free delivery on orders over a hundred dollars.

送sung3餐caan1 —— 送sung3外ngoi6賣maai6

外ngoi6賣maai6員jyun4已ji5經ging1出ceot1咗zo2發faat3，就zau6快faai3到dou3。
外卖员已经出发了，快到了。
The delivery rider has set off and will be here soon.

到dou3咗zo2樓lau4下haa6 —— 到dou3達daat6

你nei5好hou2，我ngo5到dou3咗zo2樓lau4下haa6，你nei5落lok6嚟lai4攞lo2得dak1唔m4得dak1？
你好，我到楼下了，你下来拿可以吗？
Hi, I'm downstairs, can you come down to pick it up?

放fong3喺hai2門mun4口hau2就zau6得dak1，唔m4該goi1晒saai3。
放在门口就行，谢谢。
Just leave it at the door, thanks a lot.

漏lau6咗zo2 —— 少siu2送sung3咗zo2

唔m4見gin3咗zo2杯bui1嘢je5飲jam2，佢keoi5哋dei6漏lau6咗zo2。
饮料不见了，他们漏送了。
The drink is missing, they left it out.
