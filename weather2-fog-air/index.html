Fog and Poor Air: Haze, Masks, Clear Skies

有jau5時si4大daai6霧mou6睇tai2唔m4到dou2對deoi3面min6海hoi2，有jau5時si4空hung1氣hei3差caa1要jiu3戴daai3口hau2罩zaau3。講gong2能nang4見gin3度dou6同tung4空hung1氣hei3質zat1素sou3嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Sometimes the fog hides the other side of the harbour, sometimes the air is so bad you need a mask. Cantonese for visibility and air quality, with Jyutping.

天氣進階日常會話

大daai6霧mou6 —— 大daai6雾mou6

今gam1朝ziu1大daai6霧mou6，對deoi3面min6海hoi2都dou1睇tai2唔m4到dou2。
今早大雾，连对岸都看不到。
It's so foggy this morning you can't even see across the harbour.

空hung1氣hei3差caa1 —— 空hung1气hei3质zat1量loeng6差caa1

今gam1日jat6空hung1氣hei3好hou2差caa1，出ceot1街gaai1戴daai3返faan1個go3口hau2罩zaau3。
今天空气很差，出门戴上口罩吧。
The air is really bad today, wear a mask when you go out.

煙jin1霞haa4 —— 雾mou6霾maai4

成seng4個go3天tin1灰fui1濛mung4濛mung4，應jing1該goi1係hai6煙jin1霞haa4。
整个天空灰蒙蒙的，应该是雾霾。
The whole sky is grey, it must be haze.

It's clear and fresh today, perfect for hiking.
