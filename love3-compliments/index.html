讚zaan3另ling6一jat1半bun3：好hou2襯can3、有jau5眼ngaan5光gwong1、天tin1生saang1一jat1對deoi3、留lau4言jin4

Praising Couples: Well-Matched, Good Taste, Made for Each Other, Comments

朋pang4友jau5貼tip3情cing4侶leoi5相soeng1，喺hai2下haa6面min6「留lau4言jin4」讚zaan3佢keoi5哋dei6「好hou2襯can3」、「天tin1生saang1一jat1對deoi3」，最zeoi3地dei6道dou6嘅ge3讚zaan3法faat3。

When friends post couple photos, leave a comment saying they're well-matched and made for each other.

戀愛俚語網絡

好hou2襯can3 —— 好hou2配pui3

你nei5哋dei6兩loeng5個go3企kei5埋maai4一jat1齊cai4好hou2襯can3。
你们俩站在一起很般配。
You two look great together.

有jau5眼ngaan5光gwong1 —— 揀gaan2得dak1好hou2

你nei5男naam4朋pang4友jau5好hou2有jau5眼ngaan5光gwong1。
你男朋友很有眼光。
Your boyfriend has great taste.

天tin1生saang1一jat1對deoi3 —— 好hou2夾gaap3好hou2襯can3嘅ge3一jat1對deoi3

睇tai2佢keoi5哋dei6張zoeng1相soeng2，真zan1係hai6天tin1生saang1一jat1對deoi3。
看他们的照片，真是天生一对。
Looking at their photo, they're truly made for each other.

留lau4言jin4 —— 喺hai2網mong5上soeng6寫se2評ping4論leon6

朋pang4友jau5喺hai2我ngo5哋dei6張zoeng1合hap6照ziu3下haa6面min6留lau4言jin4：「好hou2甜tim4呀aa3！」
朋友在我们的合照下面留言：「好甜啊！」
A friend commented under our photo: 'So sweet!'
