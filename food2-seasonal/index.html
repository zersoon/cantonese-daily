冬dung1天tin1打daa2邊bin1爐lou4、夏haa6天tin1食sik6糖tong4水seoi2：跟gan1住zyu6天tin1氣hei3食sik6嘢je5

Hot Pot in Winter, Sweet Soup in Summer: Eating with the Weather

香hoeng1港gong2人jan4好hou2講gong2究gau3「天tin1氣hei3食sik6嘢je5」：凍dung3就zau6煲bou1湯tong1打daa2邊bin1爐lou4，熱jit6就zau6飲jam2凍dung3飲jam2食sik6糖tong4水seoi2。相soeng1關gwaan1廣gwong2東dung1話waa2一jat1次ci3學hok6，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Hong Kong people eat by the weather: soup and hot pot when it's cold, iced drinks and sweet soups when it's hot. Learn the Cantonese, with Jyutping.

飲食天氣香港

打daa2邊bin1爐lou4 —— 食sik6火fo2鍋wo1

天tin1氣hei3咁gam3凍dung3，今gam1晚maan5去heoi3打daa2邊bin1爐lou4啦laa1。
天气这么冷，今晚去吃火锅吧。
It's so cold, let's have hot pot tonight.

煲bou1湯tong1 —— 用jung6慢maan6火fo2煮zyu2湯tong1

阿aa3媽maa1今gam1日jat6煲bou1咗zo2老lou5火fo2湯tong1，早zou2啲di1返faan1嚟lai4飲jam2。
妈妈今天煲了老火汤，早点回来喝。
Mum made slow-cooked soup today, come home early to have some.

凍dung3飲jam2 —— 冰bing1凍dung3飲jam2品ban2

熱jit6到dou3頂ding2唔m4順seon6，要jiu3杯bui1凍dung3飲jam2先sin1。
热得受不了，先来杯冷饮。
It's unbearably hot, I need a cold drink first.

糖tong4水seoi2 —— 甜tim4湯tong1、甜tim4品ban2

糖tong4水seoi2有jau5凍dung3有jau5熱jit6，例lai6如jyu4紅hung4豆dau2沙saa1、芒mong1果gwo2西sai1米mai5露lou6。

夏haa6天tin1食sik6碗wun2凍dung3嘅ge3芒mong1果gwo2西sai1米mai5露lou6，好hou2舒syu1服fuk6。
夏天吃一碗冰的芒果西米露，很舒服。
A bowl of cold mango sago in summer feels great.

熱jit6氣hei3 —— 上soeng6火fo2

天tin1氣hei3咁gam3乾gon1，唔m4好hou2食sik6咁gam3多do1煎zin1炸zaa3嘢je5，好hou2熱jit6氣hei3。
天气这么干燥，别吃那么多煎炸的东西，很上火。
The weather's so dry, don't eat so much fried food, it's "heaty".
