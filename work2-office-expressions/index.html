Everyday Office Expressions: Quitting in a Huff, Taking the Blame, Sucking Up

「劈pek3炮paau3唔m4撈lou1」、「孭me1鑊wok6」、「擦caat3鞋haai4」係hai6香hoeng1港gong2打daa2工gung1仔zai2講gong2咗zo2幾gei2十sap6年nin4都dou1仲zung6用jung6緊gan2嘅ge3講gong2法faat3，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

'Pek paau', 'me wok' and 'caat haai' are office expressions Hong Kong workers still use every day. With Jyutping.

職場俚語香港

打daa2工gung1仔zai2 —— 打daa2工gung1的dik1人jan4

打daa2工gung1仔zai2邊bin1有jau5得dak1揀gaan2。
打工的人哪有得选择。
As an employee, you don't really get a choice.

劈pek3炮paau3唔m4撈lou1 —— 一jat1氣hei3之zi1下haa6辭ci4職zik1

佢keoi5受sau6唔m4住zyu6，就zau6劈pek3炮paau3唔m4撈lou1。
他受不了，就一气之下辞职了。
He couldn't take it any more, so he quit on the spot.

孭me1鑊wok6 —— 背bui3黑hak1鍋wo1

出ceot1咗zo2事si6，冇mou5人jan4想soeng2孭me1鑊wok6。
出了事，没人想背黑锅。
When something goes wrong, nobody wants to take the blame.

擦caat3鞋haai4 —— 拍paak3馬maa5屁pei3

佢keoi5成sing4日jat6擦caat3老lou5細sai3鞋haai4。
他总是拍老板马屁。
He's always sucking up to the boss.

頂ding2硬ngaang6上soeng5 —— 硬ngaang6着zoek6頭tau4皮pei4做zou6

人jan4手sau2唔m4夠gau3，唯wai4有jau5頂ding2硬ngaang6上soeng5。
人手不够，只好硬着头皮做。
We're short-staffed, so we just have to push through.
