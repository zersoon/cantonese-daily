Buying Medicine at the Pharmacy: Cough Syrup, Fever Pills, Drowsiness

Sore throat or a fever? Learn how to ask the pharmacist for cough syrup and fever medicine, and whether it'll make you drowsy.

Is there a pharmacy near here?

I've been coughing for a few days and want a bottle of cough syrup.

Will it make me drowsy?

Three times a day, after meals.
