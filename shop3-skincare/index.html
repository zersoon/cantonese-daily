買maai5護wu6膚fu1品ban2：試si3用jung6裝zong1、乾gon1性sing3皮pei4膚fu1、敏man5感gam2、防fong4曬saai3

Buying Skincare: Samples, Dry Skin, Sensitive Skin, Sunscreen

買maai5護wu6膚fu1品ban2前cin4問man6下haa6有jau5冇mou5試si3用jung6裝zong1、適sik1唔m4適sik1合hap6乾gon1性sing3皮pei4膚fu1。護wu6膚fu1品ban2專zyun1櫃gwai6常soeng4用jung6廣gwong2東dung1話waa2。

Before buying skincare, ask for a sample and whether it suits dry or sensitive skin. Handy Cantonese for the beauty counter.

購物日常會話進階

試si3用jung6裝zong1 —— 试si3用jung6装zong1

有jau5冇mou5試si3用jung6裝zong1可ho2以ji5畀bei2我ngo5試si3下haa5？
有没有试用装可以给我试试？
Do you have a sample I could try?

乾gon1性sing3皮pei4膚fu1 —— 干gon1性sing3皮pei4肤fu1

我ngo5係hai6乾gon1性sing3皮pei4膚fu1，用jung6邊bin1隻zek3面min6霜soeng1好hou2？
我是干性皮肤，用哪款面霜好？
I have dry skin. Which face cream should I use?

敏man5感gam2 —— 敏man5感gam2、过gwo3敏man5

我ngo5皮pei4膚fu1好hou2敏man5感gam2，一jat1用jung6錯co3嘢je5就zau6出ceot1紅hung4點dim2。
我皮肤很敏感，一用错东西就起红点。
My skin is very sensitive; the wrong product gives me red spots.

防fong4曬saai3 —— 防fong4晒saai3

夏haa6天tin1出ceot1街gaai1記gei3得dak1搽caa4防fong4曬saai3。
夏天出门记得涂防晒。
Remember to put on sunscreen when you go out in summer.
