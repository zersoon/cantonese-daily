Beach Day: Swimming, Sunscreen, Lifeguards, the Red Flag

夏haa6天tin1天tin1朗long5氣hei3清cing1，最zeoi3啱ngaam1去heoi3海hoi2灘taan1游jau4水seoi2。學hok6識sik1搽caa4太taai3陽joeng4油jau4、救gau3生sang1員jyun4同tung4掛gwaa3紅hung4旗kei4，玩waan2得dak1開hoi1心sam1又jau6安on1全cyun4。附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Clear summer days are perfect for the beach. Learn 'put on sunscreen', 'lifeguard' and 'red flag' so you stay safe while having fun. With Jyutping.

交通天氣香港

游jau4水seoi2 —— 游jau4泳wing6

今gam1日jat6咁gam3好hou2天tin1，不bat1如jyu4去heoi3淺cin2水seoi2灣waan1游jau4水seoi2。
今天天气这么好，不如去浅水湾游泳。
It's such a nice day, let's go swimming at Repulse Bay.

搽caa4太taai3陽joeng4油jau4 —— 涂tou4防fong4晒saai3霜soeng1

落lok6水seoi2之zi1前cin4記gei3得dak1搽caa4太taai3陽joeng4油jau4，唔m4係hai6會wui5曬saai3傷soeng1。
下水前记得涂防晒霜，不然会晒伤。
Put on sunscreen before you go in the water, or you'll get sunburnt.

救gau3生sang1員jyun4 —— 救gau3生sang1员jyun4

喺hai2有jau5救gau3生sang1員jyun4嘅ge3範faan6圍wai4入jap6面min6游jau4，安on1全cyun4啲di1。
在有救生员的范围里面游，安全一点。
Swim inside the area with lifeguards, it's safer.

掛gwaa3紅hung4旗kei4 —— 挂gwaa3红hung4旗kei4（不bat1宜ji4游jau4泳wing6）

海hoi2灘taan1掛gwaa3咗zo2紅hung4旗kei4，今gam1日jat6唔m4好hou2落lok6水seoi2。
海滩挂了红旗，今天别下水。
The red flag is up at the beach, don't go in the water today.

搭daap3巴baa1士si2入jap6去heoi3 —— 坐zo6公gung1交gaau1车ce1进zeon3去heoi3

假gaa3期kei4泊paak3車ce1位wai2好hou2少siu2，搭daap3巴baa1士si2入jap6去heoi3方fong1便bin6啲di1。
假期停车位很少，坐公交车进去方便一点。
Parking is scarce on holidays, it's easier to take the bus there.
