出ceot1差caai1遇jyu6上soeng5壞waai6天tin1氣hei3：航hong4班baan1取ceoi2消siu1、改goi2期kei4、轉zyun3機gei1、喺hai2機gei1場coeng4等dang2

Bad Weather on a Business Trip: Cancelled Flights, Rebooking, Connections, Waiting at the Airport

去heoi3出ceot1差caai1點dim2知zi1天tin1氣hei3唔m4好hou2，「航hong4班baan1取ceoi2消siu1」要jiu3「改goi2期kei4」，「轉zyun3機gei1」又jau6趕gon2唔m4切cit3。學hok6識sik1呢ne1幾gei2句geoi3，喺hai2機gei1場coeng4都dou1識sik1得dak1應jing3付fu6。

Off on a business trip and the weather turns bad: your flight is cancelled, you need to rebook, and you miss your connection. Learn the phrases to handle it.

職場交通天氣

航hong4班baan1 —— 班baan1機gei1

因jan1為wai6打daa2風fung1，今gam1晚maan5啲di1航hong4班baan1全cyun4部bou6取ceoi2消siu1。
因为台风，今晚的航班全部取消。
Because of the typhoon, all flights tonight are cancelled.

改goi2期kei4 —— 更gang1改goi2日jat6期kei4

我ngo5要jiu3將zoeng1機gei1票piu3改goi2期kei4，改goi2去heoi3聽ting1朝ziu1。
我要把机票改期，改到明天早上。
I need to change my ticket to tomorrow morning.

轉zyun3機gei1 —— 轉zyun2乘sing4飛fei1機gei1

班baan1機gei1遲ci4咗zo2起hei2飛fei1，我ngo5驚geng1趕gon2唔m4切cit3轉zyun3機gei1。
航班晚了起飞，我怕赶不上转机。
The flight took off late, I'm afraid I'll miss my connection.

喺hai2機gei1場coeng4等dang2 —— 在zoi6機gei1場coeng4等dang2候hau6

大daai6霧mou6，我ngo5哋dei6喺hai2機gei1場coeng4等dang2咗zo2成seng4晚maan5。
大雾，我们在机场等了一整晚。
It was foggy, and we waited at the airport all night.
