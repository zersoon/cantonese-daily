買maai5海hoi2鮮sin1：幾gei2多do1錢cin2一jat1斤gan1、生saang1猛maang5、劏tong1埋maai4

Buying Seafood: Price per Catty, Live and Kicking, Cleaned for You

買maai5魚jyu4買maai5蝦haa1要jiu3識sik1問man6重cung5量loeng6同tung4價gaa3錢cin4：「幾gei2多do1錢cin2一jat1斤gan1」「幾gei2多do1兩loeng2」。學hok6識sik1斤gan1兩loeng2嘅ge3講gong2法faat3同tung4叫giu3魚jyu4檔dong3幫bong1你nei5劏tong1魚jyu4，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Buying fish and prawns means asking about weight and price: per catty, how many taels. Learn weight expressions and how to get the fishmonger to clean your fish, with Jyutping.

飲食購物語法

幾gei2多do1錢cin2一jat1斤gan1 —— 問man6單daan1位wai2價gaa3錢cin4

句geoi3式sik1：「幾gei2多do1錢cin2 + 一jat1 + 量loeng6詞ci4」。斤gan1之zi1外ngoi6，都dou1可ho2以ji5講gong2一jat1磅bong6、一jat1隻zek3。

呢ni1啲di1蝦haa1幾gei2多do1錢cin2一jat1斤gan1？
这些虾多少钱一斤？
How much are these prawns per catty?

兩loeng2 —— 一jat1斤gan1嘅ge3十sap6六luk6分fan1之zi1一jat1

「幾gei2多do1兩loeng2」用jung6嚟lai4問man6重cung5量loeng6，數sou3字zi6讀duk6 loeng2，唔m4係hai6 loeng5。

呢ni1條tiu4魚jyu2有jau5幾gei2多do1兩loeng2？
这条鱼有几两重？
How many taels does this fish weigh?

斤gan1四sei3兩loeng2，一jat1百baak3二ji6蚊man1。
一斤四两，一百二十块。
One catty four taels, a hundred and twenty dollars.

生saang1猛maang5 —— 仲zung6生saang1勾ngau1勾ngau1

啲di1蟹haai5好hou2生saang1猛maang5，仲zung6識sik1郁juk1。
螃蟹很鲜活，还会动。
The crabs are really lively, they're still moving.

劏tong1 —— 殺saat3魚jyu4、清cing1內noi6臟zong6

唔m4該goi1幫bong1我ngo5劏tong1埋maai4，切cit3開hoi1兩loeng5邊bin1。
麻烦帮我杀好，切成两半。
Please clean it for me and cut it in half.
