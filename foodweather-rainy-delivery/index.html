落lok6雨jyu5唔m4想soeng2出ceot1街gaai1：叫giu3外ngoi6賣maai6、加gaa1運wan6費fai3、遲ci4咗zo2到dou3

Rainy Day, Stay In: Ordering Takeaway, Extra Fees and Late Deliveries

落lok6大daai6雨jyu5唔m4想soeng2出ceot1門mun4口hau2，好hou2多do1人jan4都dou1會wui5喺hai2手sau2機gei1叫giu3外ngoi6賣maai6。學hok6吓haa2落lok6雨jyu5天tin1叫giu3外ngoi6賣maai6常soeng4用jung6嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

When it pours, many people order food on their phones. Learn the Cantonese for rainy-day delivery, with Jyutping.

飲食天氣網絡

落lok6大daai6雨jyu5 —— 下haa6大daai6雨jyu5

出ceot1面min6落lok6緊gan2大daai6雨jyu5，不bat1如jyu4叫giu3外ngoi6賣maai6啦laa1。
外面正在下大雨，不如叫外卖吧。
It's pouring outside, why don't we order takeaway.

運wan6費fai3 —— 配pui3送sung3费fai3

落lok6雨jyu5運wan6費fai3貴gwai3咗zo2，不bat1過gwo3都dou1抵dai2過gwo3自zi6己gei2去heoi3攞lo2。
下雨配送费贵了，不过还是比自己去拿划算。
Delivery costs more in the rain, but it's still worth more than going yourself.

遲ci4咗zo2到dou3 —— 晚maan5到dou3了liu5

個go3 app 話waa6落lok6雨jyu5，可ho2能nang4會wui5遲ci4咗zo2到dou3。
应用程序说下雨了，可能会晚点送到。
The app says it's raining, so it might arrive late.

湯tong1瀉se3晒saai3 —— 汤tong1全cyun4洒sai2了liu5

送sung3嚟lai4嗰go2陣zan6湯tong1瀉se3晒saai3，袋doi2都dou1濕sap1埋maai4。
送来的时候汤全洒了，袋子也湿了。
The soup had spilled everywhere and the bag was soaked too.

畀bei2貼tip1士si2 —— 给kap1小siu2费fai3

佢keoi5冒mou6雨jyu5送sung3餐caan1好hou2辛san1苦fu2，畀bei2多do1少siu2少siu2貼tip1士si2啦laa1。
他冒雨送餐很辛苦，多给一点小费吧。
He delivered in the rain, that's hard work, let's give a bit of a tip.
