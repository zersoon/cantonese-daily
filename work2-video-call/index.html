視si6像zoeng6會wui6議ji5廣gwong2東dung1話waa2：開hoi1咪mai1、開hoi1鏡geng3頭tau4、斷tyun5線sin3、分fan1享hoeng2畫waa6面min6

Video Meetings in Cantonese: Unmute, Camera On, Dropped Call, Share Screen

喺hai2屋uk1企kei2用jung6 Zoom 開hoi1會wui2，最zeoi3常soeng4講gong2嘅ge3就zau6係hai6「你nei5未mei6開hoi1咪mai1呀aa3」。視si6像zoeng6會wui6議ji5實sat6用jung6廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

On a Zoom call from home, the line you'll hear most is 'you're still on mute'. Practical Cantonese for video meetings, with Jyutping.

職場網絡入門

開hoi1咪mai1 / 閂saan1咪mai1 —— 開hoi1麥mak6 / 靜zing6音jam1

你nei5未mei6開hoi1咪mai1呀aa3，我ngo5哋dei6聽teng1唔m4到dou2你nei5。
你还没开麦，我们听不到你。
You're still on mute, we can't hear you.

開hoi1鏡geng3頭tau4 —— 開hoi1攝sip3像zoeng6頭tau4

大daai6家gaa1可ho2唔m4可ho2以ji5開hoi1鏡geng3頭tau4？
大家可不可以开摄像头？
Could everyone turn their cameras on?

斷tyun5線sin3 —— 掉diu6線sin3

頭tau4先sin1我ngo5斷tyun5咗zo2線sin3，你nei5可ho2唔m4可ho2以ji5講gong2多do1次ci3？
刚才我掉线了，你可以再说一次吗？
I got cut off just now, could you say that again?

分fan1享hoeng2畫waa6面min6 —— 共gung6享hoeng2屏ping4幕mok6

我ngo5而ji4家gaa1分fan1享hoeng2畫waa6面min6，你nei5哋dei6睇tai2唔m4睇tai2到dou2？
我现在共享屏幕，你们看得到吗？
I'm sharing my screen now, can you all see it?

我ngo5將zoeng1 Zoom 連lin4結kit3放fong3咗zo2喺hai2群kwan4組zou2。
我把 Zoom 链接放在群里了。
I've put the Zoom link in the group.
