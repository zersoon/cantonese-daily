Autumn Barbecue in Hong Kong: BBQ Sites, Forks and Burnt Food

天tin1氣hei3轉zyun2涼loeng4，香hoeng1港gong2人jan4最zeoi3鍾zung1意ji3約joek3埋maai4朋pang4友jau5去heoi3燒siu1烤haau1場coeng4燒siu1嘢je5食sik6。燒siu1烤haau1用jung6語jyu5一jat1次ci3學hok6，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

When it cools down, Hong Kong people love a barbecue with friends at a BBQ site. Learn the barbecue vocabulary, with Jyutping.

飲食天氣香港

燒siu1嘢je5食sik6 —— 烧siu1烤haau1

天tin1氣hei3涼loeng4咗zo2，星sing1期kei4六luk6去heoi3西sai1貢gung3燒siu1嘢je5食sik6好hou2唔m4好hou2？
天气凉了，星期六去西贡烧烤好不好？
It's cooler now, how about a barbecue in Sai Kung on Saturday?

燒siu1烤haau1叉caa1 —— 烧siu1烤haau1叉caa1

每mui5人jan4攞lo2一jat1支zi1燒siu1烤haau1叉caa1，串cyun3住zyu6雞gai1翼jik6慢maan6慢maan2燒siu1。
每人拿一支烧烤叉，串着鸡翅慢慢烤。
Everyone takes a fork, skewers a chicken wing and grills it slowly.

搽caa4蜜mat6糖tong4 —— 涂tou4蜂fung1蜜mat6

就zau6熟suk6嗰go2陣zan6搽caa4啲di1蜜mat6糖tong4，會wui5靚leng3色sik1啲di1。
快熟的时候涂点蜂蜜，颜色会好看一点。
Brush on some honey when it's nearly done, it'll look nicer.

燶nung1咗zo2 —— 烤haau1焦ziu1了liu5

風fung1太taai3大daai6，火fo2好hou2猛maang5，條tiu4腸coeng2仔zai2燶nung1咗zo2。
风太大，火很旺，香肠烤焦了。
It was too windy, the fire got fierce and the sausage burnt.

生saang1火fo2 —— 点dim2火fo2

今gam1日jat6好hou2潮ciu4濕sap1，啲di1炭taan3好hou2難naan4生saang1火fo2。
今天很潮湿，炭很难点着。
It's humid today, the charcoal is hard to light.
