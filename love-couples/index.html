情cing4侶leoi5日jat6常soeng4：呷haap3醋cou3、氹tam3、嗌aai3交gaau1、冷laang5戰zin3

Couple Life: Jealousy, Coaxing, Fighting, the Silent Treatment

拍paak3拖to1之zi1後hau6日jat6日jat6都dou1會wui5用jung6到dou3：另ling6一jat1半bun3「呷haap3醋cou3」要jiu3識sik1「氹tam3」，「嗌aai3交gaau1」之zi1後hau6唔m4好hou2「冷laang5戰zin3」。情cing4侶leoi5之zi1間gaan1最zeoi3常soeng4用jung6嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Words you'll need once you're together: when your partner gets jealous, you coax them; after a fight, avoid the silent treatment. Everyday Cantonese for couples, with Jyutping.

俚語戀愛鬧人嗌交

呷haap3醋cou3 —— 吃hek3醋cou3

我ngo5同tung4女neoi5同tung4事si6食sik6個go3飯faan6，佢keoi5都dou1呷haap3醋cou3。
我跟女同事吃个饭，她都吃醋。
I just had lunch with a female colleague and she got jealous.

氹tam3 —— 哄hung3

佢keoi5嬲nau1咗zo2，你nei5快faai3啲di1去heoi3氹tam3返faan1佢keoi5啦laa1。
她生气了，你快点去哄她吧。
She's upset — go and cheer her up, quick.

嗌aai3交gaau1 —— 吵caau2架gaa3

佢keoi5哋dei6為wai6咗zo2啲di1小siu2事si6嗌aai3交gaau1。
他们为了些小事吵架。
They fought over something trivial.

冷laang5戰zin3 —— 唔m4講gong2嘢je5、唔m4理lei5對deoi3方fong1

佢keoi5哋dei6冷laang5戰zin3咗zo2成seng4個go3星sing1期kei4。
他们冷战了整整一个星期。
They gave each other the silent treatment for a whole week.

二ji6人jan4世sai3界gaai3 —— 兩loeng5個go3人jan4單daan1獨duk6相soeng1處cyu3

今gam1晚maan5唔m4好hou2搵wan2我ngo5，我ngo5要jiu3過gwo3二ji6人jan4世sai3界gaai3。
今晚别找我，我要过二人世界。
Don't call me tonight, it's date night for just the two of us.
