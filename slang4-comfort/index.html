安on1慰wai3人jan4點dim2講gong2：冇mou5事si6嘅ge3、唔m4使sai2驚geng1、加gaa1油jau2、會wui5好hou2返faan1

Comforting Someone: It's Fine, Don't Worry, Keep Going, It'll Get Better

朋pang4友jau5唔m4開hoi1心sam1，一jat1句geoi3「冇mou5事si6嘅ge3」已ji5經ging1好hou2暖nyun5。學hok6識sik1幾gei2句geoi3安on1慰wai3同tung4打daa2氣hei3嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，關gwaan1心sam1人jan4更gang3自zi6然jin4。

When a friend is down, 'mou5 si6 ge3' goes a long way. Learn warm Cantonese phrases for comfort and encouragement.

俚語日常會話戀愛

冇mou5事si6嘅ge3 —— 冇mou5問man6題tai4、唔m4使sai2擔daam1心sam1

冇mou5事si6嘅ge3，下haa6次ci3再zoi3試si3過gwo3。
没事的，下次再试。
It's fine, just try again next time.

唔m4使sai2驚geng1 —— 唔m4好hou2怕paa3

唔m4使sai2驚geng1，我ngo5陪pui4你nei5去heoi3。
别怕，我陪你去。
Don't worry, I'll go with you.

加gaa1油jau2 —— 努nou5力lik6、撐caang1住zyu6

聽ting1日jat6考haau2試si5，加gaa1油jau2呀aa3！
明天考试，加油啊！
Exam tomorrow — you've got this!

口hau2語jyu5「加gaa1油jau2」嘅ge3「油jau4」多do1數sou3讀duk6高gou1升sing1調diu6 jau2。

會wui5好hou2返faan1嘅ge3 —— 會wui5變bin3好hou2

唔m4好hou2諗nam2咁gam3多do1，會wui5好hou2返faan1嘅ge3。
别想太多，会好起来的。
Don't overthink it, things will get better.
