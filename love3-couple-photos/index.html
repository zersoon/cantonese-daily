Taking photos together, selfies, editing, and matching profile pictures: Cantonese for couple photos online.

影jing2合hap6照ziu3 —— 一jat1齊cai4影jing2相soeng2

我ngo5哋dei6去heoi3旅leoi5行hang4，影jing2咗zo2好hou2多do1合hap6照ziu3。

We took lots of photos together on our trip.

He held up his phone to take a selfie.

They're using matching profile pictures.
