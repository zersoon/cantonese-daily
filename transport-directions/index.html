問man6路lou6：點dim2去heoi3、直zik6行haang4、轉zyun3左zo2轉zyun3右jau6、過gwo3馬maa5路lou6

Asking for Directions: How to Get There, Straight On, Left and Right, Crossing the Road

喺hai2香hoeng1港gong2蕩dong6失sat1路lou6點dim2問man6人jan4？「轉zyun3左zo2」「轉zyun3右jau6」點dim2講gong2？問man6路lou6同tung4指zi2路lou6最zeoi3實sat6用jung6嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Lost in Hong Kong? How do you say turn left or right? The most practical Cantonese for asking and giving directions, with Jyutping.

交通日常會話入門

點dim2去heoi3 —— 怎zam2麼mo1去heoi3

唔m4該goi1，請cing2問man6地dei6鐵tit3站zaam6點dim2去heoi3？
请问地铁站怎么走？
Excuse me, how do I get to the MTR station?

直zik6行haang4 —— 一jat1直zik6走zau2

一jat1直zik6行haang4，見gin3到dou2紅hung4綠luk6燈dang1就zau6到dou3。
一直走，看到红绿灯就到了。
Go straight, and you're there when you see the traffic lights.

轉zyun3左zo2 / 轉zyun3右jau6

過gwo3咗zo2馬maa5路lou6之zi1後hau6轉zyun3右jau6。
过了马路之后右转。
Turn right after crossing the road.

行haang4幾gei2耐noi6 —— 要jiu3走zau2多do1久gau2

由jau4呢ni1度dou6行haang4過gwo3去heoi3要jiu3幾gei2耐noi6？
从这里走过去要多久？
How long does it take to walk there from here?
