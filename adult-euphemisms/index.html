Hong Kongers usually talk about intimacy indirectly. A few expressions you'll hear between friends, and how to use them without awkwardness, with Jyutping.

過gwo3夜je6 —— 喺hai2對deoi3方fong1屋uk1企kei2留lau4宿suk1

She stayed the night at her boyfriend's place.

上soeng5床cong4 —— 發faat3生sang1關gwaan1係hai6（含ham4蓄cuk1講gong2法faat3）

字zi6面min2係hai6「上soeng5床cong4」，喺hai2呢ni1個go3語jyu5境ging2係hai6指zi2發faat3生sang1親can1密mat6關gwaan1係hai6，語jyu5氣hei3中zung1性sing3，唔m4算syun3粗cou1俗zuk6。

佢keoi5哋dei6識sik1咗zo2冇mou5耐noi6就zau6上soeng5床cong4。
他们认识没多久就上床了。
They slept together not long after they met.

一jat1夜je6情cing4 —— 只zi2係hai6一jat1晚maan5嘅ge3關gwaan1係hai6

佢keoi5唔m4想soeng2搞gaau2一jat1夜je6情cing4，想soeng2認jing6真zan1拍paak3拖to1。
她不想搞一夜情，想认真谈恋爱。
She doesn't want a one-night stand; she wants a real relationship.
