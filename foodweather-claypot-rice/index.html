凍dung3啲di1就zau6想soeng2食sik6熱jit6嘢je5：煲bou1仔zai2飯faan6、飯faan6焦ziu1、趁can3熱jit6食sik6

Colder Days, Hotter Food: Claypot Rice and Comparing with 'a Bit More'

天tin1氣hei3一jat1凍dung3，大daai6家gaa1就zau6想soeng2食sik6煲bou1仔zai2飯faan6。順seon6便bin2學hok6「凍dung3啲di1」「熱jit6啲di1」「…過gwo3…」比bei2較gaau3句geoi3，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

When it gets cold, everyone craves claypot rice. Learn comparisons like 'a bit colder', 'a bit hotter' and '…-er than…', with Jyutping.

飲食天氣語法

凍dung3啲di1 —— 冷laang5一jat1点dim2

形jing4容jung4詞ci4後hau6面min6加gaa1「啲di1」，就zau6係hai6「…一jat1點dim2」。

今gam1日jat6凍dung3啲di1，食sik6煲bou1仔zai2飯faan6啱ngaam1晒saai3。
今天冷一点，吃煲仔饭正合适。
It's a bit colder today, perfect for claypot rice.

凍dung3過gwo3 —— 比bei2……冷laang5

今gam1晚maan5凍dung3過gwo3琴kam4晚maan5，要jiu3個go3臘laap6味mei6煲bou1仔zai2飯faan6先sin1。
今晚比昨晚冷，先来一个腊味煲仔饭。
Tonight is colder than last night, I'll have a cured-meat claypot rice.

飯faan6焦ziu1 —— 锅wo1巴baa1

煲bou1仔zai2飯faan6最zeoi3好hou2食sik6就zau6係hai6啲di1飯faan6焦ziu1。
煲仔饭最好吃的就是锅巴。
The best part of claypot rice is the crispy crust.

趁can3熱jit6食sik6 —— 趁can3热jit6吃hek3

出ceot1面min6好hou2大daai6風fung1，啲di1飯faan6好hou2快faai3凍dung3，快faai3啲di1趁can3熱jit6食sik6。
外面风很大，饭很快就凉，快点趁热吃。
It's windy out and the rice cools fast, eat it while it's hot.

熱jit6啲di1 —— 热jit6一jat1点dim2

碗wun2湯tong1唔m4夠gau3熱jit6，唔m4該goi1幫bong1我ngo5翻faan1熱jit6啲di1。
这碗汤不够热，麻烦帮我再加热一下。
The soup isn't hot enough, could you heat it up a bit more please.

詞 粵拼 意思 English
