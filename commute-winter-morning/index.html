冬dung1天tin1朝ziu1早zou2出ceot1門mun4：起hei2身san1、瞓fan3過gwo3龍lung4、着zoek3多do1件gin6、熱jit6飲jam2

Winter Mornings Before Work: Getting Up, Oversleeping, Wearing an Extra Layer, a Hot Drink

天tin1氣hei3一jat1涼loeng4，朝ziu1早zou2最zeoi3難naan4「起hei2身san1」，一jat1唔m4小siu2心sam1就zau6「瞓fan3過gwo3龍lung4」。記gei3得dak1「着zoek3多do1件gin6」，喺hai2車ce1站zaam6買maai5杯bui1「熱jit6飲jam2」先sin1返faan2公gung1司si1。

When it turns cold, getting up is the hardest part, and it's easy to oversleep. Wear an extra layer and grab a hot drink on the way to the office.

職場交通天氣

起hei2身san1 —— 起hei2床cong4

咁gam3凍dung3，朝ziu1早zou2真zan1係hai6唔m4想soeng2起hei2身san1。
这么冷，早上真的不想起床。
It's so cold, I really don't want to get up in the morning.

瞓fan3過gwo3龍lung4 —— 睡seoi6過gwo3頭tau4

我ngo5今gam1朝ziu1瞓fan3過gwo3龍lung4，趕gon2唔m4切cit3搭daap3小siu2巴baa1。
我今天早上睡过头，赶不上坐小巴。
I overslept this morning and missed the minibus.

着zoek3多do1件gin6 —— 多do1穿cyun1一jat1件gin6

車ce1站zaam6好hou2大daai6風fung1，出ceot1門mun4着zoek3多do1件gin6啦laa1。
车站风很大，出门多穿一件吧。
It's windy at the station, put on an extra layer.

熱jit6飲jam2 —— 熱jit6的dik1飲jam2品ban2

等dang2車ce1嗰go2陣zan6，買maai5杯bui1熱jit6飲jam2暖nyun5下haa5。
等车的时候，买杯热饮暖一暖。
While waiting for the bus, buy a hot drink to warm up.
