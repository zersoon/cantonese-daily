情cing4侶leoi5傳cyun4訊seon3息sik1：報bou3平ping4安on1、即zik1刻hak1覆fuk1、打daa2畀bei2我ngo5、錄luk6音jam1

Couple Messaging: Checking In, Replying Right Away, Call Me, Voice Messages

拍paak3拖to1之zi1後hau6日jat6日jat6傳cyun4訊seon3息sik1：返faan2到dou3屋uk1企kei2要jiu3「報bou3平ping4安on1」，對deoi3方fong1「即zik1刻hak1覆fuk1」你nei5就zau6最zeoi3開hoi1心sam1。

Daily messaging for couples: letting them know you got home safe, quick replies, calls and voice messages.

戀愛俚語網絡

報bou3平ping4安on1 —— 話waa6畀bei2人jan4知zi1自zi6己gei2平ping4安on1

到dou3咗zo2屋uk1企kei2記gei3得dak1報bou3平ping4安on1。
到家了记得报个平安。
Let me know when you get home safe.

即zik1刻hak1覆fuk1 —— 好hou2快faai3回wui4覆fuk1

佢keoi5每mui5次ci3都dou1即zik1刻hak1覆fuk1我ngo5。
他每次都马上回复我。
He always replies to me right away.

打daa2畀bei2我ngo5 —— 打daa2電din6話waa2畀bei2我ngo5

得dak1閒haan4打daa2畀bei2我ngo5，想soeng2聽teng1吓haa5你nei5把baa2聲seng1。
有空打给我，想听听你的声音。
Call me when you're free, I want to hear your voice.

錄luk6音jam1 —— 傳cyun4語jyu5音jam1訊seon3息sik1

我ngo5唔m4方fong1便bin6打daa2字zi6，錄luk6音jam1畀bei2你nei5啦laa1。
我不方便打字，给你发语音吧。
I can't type right now, I'll send you a voice message.
