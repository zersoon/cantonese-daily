買maai5文man4具geoi6：原jyun4子zi2筆bat1、單daan1行hong4簿bou2、膠gaau1紙zi2、影jing2印jan3

Buying Stationery: Ballpoint Pens, Lined Notebooks, Tape, Photocopying

開hoi1學hok6前cin4要jiu3買maai5文man4具geoi6？原jyun4子zi2筆bat1、單daan1行hong4簿bou2、膠gaau1紙zi2、影jing2印jan3，文man4具geoi6舖pou3常soeng4用jung6廣gwong2東dung1話waa2一jat1次ci3過gwo3學hok6。

Getting ready for school? Learn the Cantonese for ballpoint pens, lined exercise books, sticky tape and photocopying.

購物入門日常會話

原jyun4子zi2筆bat1 —— 圆jyun4珠zyu1笔bat1

有jau5冇mou5藍laam4色sik1原jyun4子zi2筆bat1？
有没有蓝色圆珠笔？
Do you have blue ballpoint pens?

單daan1行hong4簿bou2 —— 单daan1行haang4练lin6习zaap6本bun2

我ngo5要jiu3三saam1本bun2單daan1行hong4簿bou2。
我要三本单行练习本。
I'd like three lined exercise books.

膠gaau1紙zi2 —— 胶gaau1带daai3

膠gaau1紙zi2用jung6晒saai3，要jiu3買maai5過gwo3一jat1卷gyun2。
胶带用完了，要再买一卷。
The tape's run out, I need to buy another roll.

影jing2印jan3 —— 复fuk6印jan3

呢ni1度dou6影jing2印jan3幾gei2錢cin2一jat1張zoeng1？
这里复印多少钱一张？
How much per page to photocopy here?
