寫se2工gung1作zok3電din6郵jau4：附fu6件gin2、轉zyun2寄gei3、回wui4覆fuk1全cyun4部bou6、收sau1件gin2人jan4

Work Email Words: Attachment, Forward, Reply All, Recipient

寫se2電din6郵jau4都dou1有jau5好hou2多do1廣gwong2東dung1話waa2講gong2法faat3：「附fu6件gin2」、「轉zyun2寄gei3」、「回wui4覆fuk1全cyun4部bou6」、「收sau1件gin2人jan4」，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Talking about work emails in Cantonese: attachment, forward, reply all and recipient. With Jyutping.

職場俚語進階

附fu6件gin2 —— 電din6郵jau4附fu6上soeng5嘅ge3檔dong2案on3

個go3附fu6件gin2好hou2似ci5漏lau6咗zo2，可ho2唔m4可ho2以ji5再zoi3寄gei3過gwo3？
附件好像漏了，可不可以再发一次？
The attachment seems to be missing, could you resend it?

轉zyun2寄gei3 —— 轉zyun2發faat3

我ngo5轉zyun2寄gei3咗zo2份fan6文man4件gin2畀bei2你nei5。
我把文件转发给你了。
I've forwarded the document to you.

回wui4覆fuk1全cyun4部bou6 —— 回wui4覆fuk1所so2有jau5人jan4

回wui4覆fuk1全cyun4部bou6之zi1前cin4，睇tai2清cing1楚co2收sau1件gin2人jan4。
回复全部之前，看清楚收件人。
Check the recipients before you hit reply all.

收sau1件gin2人jan4 —— 收sau1信seon3嘅ge3人jan4

收sau1件gin2人jan4打daa2錯co3咗zo2，封fung1信seon3冇mou5寄gei3到dou3。
收件人写错了，邮件没发到。
The recipient was typed wrong, so the email didn't go through.
