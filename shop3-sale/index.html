Mall Sales: Clearance, Storewide, 30% Off, In Demand

年nin4中zung1大daai6減gaam2價gaa3，全cyun4場coeng4七cat1折zit3、清cing1貨fo3價gaa3！學hok6識sik1呢ni1啲di1詞ci4，行haang4商soeng1場coeng4睇tai2得dak1明ming4減gaam2價gaa3牌paai4。

Mid-year sales, 30% off storewide, clearance prices! Learn these words to read sale signs at the mall.

購物香港入門

清cing1貨fo3 —— 清cing1仓cong1

間gaan1舖pou3下haa6個go3月jyut6執zap1笠lap1，而ji4家gaa1清cing1貨fo3。
这家店下个月关门，现在清仓。
The shop is closing next month, so it's a clearance sale now.

全cyun4場coeng4 —— 全cyun4场coeng4

全cyun4場coeng4貨fo3品ban2一jat1律leot6半bun3價gaa3。
全场商品一律半价。
Everything in the store is half price.

七cat1折zit3 —— 打daa2七cat1折zit3（=30% off）

呢ni1件gin6褸lau1七cat1折zit3，即zik1係hai6慳haan1三saam1成sing4。
这件外套打七折，就是省三成。
This coat is 30% off.

搶coeng2手sau2 —— 抢coeng2手sau2

呢ni1款fun2好hou2搶coeng2手sau2，得dak1返faan1兩loeng5件gin6。
这款很抢手，只剩两件了。
This style is in high demand; only two left.
