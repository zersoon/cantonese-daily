凍dung3天tin1上soeng6大daai6帽mou6山saan1：結git3霜soeng1、封fung1路lou6、着zoek3多do1件gin6衫saam1

Up Tai Mo Shan in the Cold: Frost, Road Closures, Wearing Extra Layers

每mui5逢fung4寒hon4冷laang5天tin1氣hei3，好hou2多do1人jan4去heoi3大daai6帽mou6山saan1睇tai2結git3霜soeng1，但daan6路lou6面min2可ho2能nang4結git3冰bing1、甚sam6至zi3封fung1路lou6。學hok6識sik1相soeng1關gwaan1講gong2法faat3，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Whenever it gets very cold, crowds head up Tai Mo Shan to see frost, but roads can ice over or even close. Learn the words you need. With Jyutping.

結git3霜soeng1 —— 结git3霜soeng1

天tin1文man4台toi4話waa6山saan1頂deng2可ho2能nang4會wui5結git3霜soeng1。
天文台说山顶可能会结霜。
The Observatory says there may be frost on the hilltops.

路lou6面min6結git3冰bing1 —— 路lou6面min2结git3冰bing1

路lou6面min6結git3冰bing1，行haang4路lou6要jiu3好hou2小siu2心sam1，好hou2易ji6跣sin3親can1。
路面结冰，走路要很小心，很容易滑倒。
The ground is icy, walk carefully, it's easy to slip.

封fung1路lou6 —— 封fung1路lou6

上soeng5山saan1條tiu4路lou6封fung1咗zo2，啲di1車ce1上soeng5唔m4到dou2去heoi3。
上山的路封了，车子上不去。
The road up the hill is closed, cars can't get up.

着zoek3多do1件gin6衫saam1 —— 多do1穿cyun1件gin6衣ji1服fuk6

山saan1上soeng6好hou2大daai6風fung1，記gei3得dak1着zoek3多do1件gin6衫saam1。
山上风很大，记得多穿件衣服。
It's very windy up there, remember to wear an extra layer.
