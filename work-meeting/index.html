開hoi1會wui2必bit1識sik1：跟gan1進zeon3、執zap1手sau2尾mei5、卸se3膊bok3、補bou2鑊wok6

Meeting Must-Knows: Follow Up, Clean Up, Pass the Buck, Fix the Mess

開hoi1會wui2時si4人jan4人jan4都dou1講gong2「跟gan1進zeon3」，出ceot1咗zo2事si6就zau6有jau5人jan4「卸se3膊bok3」，最zeoi3後hau6要jiu3你nei5「執zap1手sau2尾mei5」。香hoeng1港gong2辦baan6公gung1室sat1開hoi1會wui2最zeoi3常soeng4聽teng1到dou3嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

In meetings everyone says they'll 'follow up', someone 'drops the shoulder' when things go wrong, and you end up 'tidying the tail'. The Cantonese you'll hear in Hong Kong meetings, with Jyutping.

職場俚語進階

跟gan1進zeon3 —— 繼gai3續zuk6處cyu5理lei5

呢ni1件gin6事si6我ngo5會wui5跟gan1進zeon3。
这件事我会跟进。
I'll follow up on this.

執zap1手sau2尾mei5 —— 幫bong1人jan4收sau1拾sap6殘caan4局guk6

佢keoi5走zau2咗zo2，要jiu3我ngo5幫bong1佢keoi5執zap1手sau2尾mei5。
他走了，要我帮他收拾烂摊子。
He left, and I have to clean up after him.

卸se3膊bok3 —— 推teoi1卸se3責zaak3任jam6

出ceot1咗zo2事si6佢keoi5就zau6卸se3膊bok3。
出了事他就推卸责任。
As soon as something goes wrong, he passes the buck.

補bou2鑊wok6 —— 補bou2救gau3錯co3誤ng6

「甩lat1鑊wok6」係hai6出ceot1錯co3，「補bou2鑊wok6」就zau6係hai6補bou2救gau3。

份fan6報bou3告gou3寫se2錯co3咗zo2，要jiu3即zik1刻hak1補bou2鑊wok6。
报告写错了，要马上补救。
The report has mistakes, we need to fix it right away.
