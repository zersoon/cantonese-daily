Signal No. 3 is up: do you still go in? When does work resume after the signal is lowered? Learn how to talk about typhoon days with colleagues.

三saam1號hou6波bo1 —— 三saam1號hou6風fung1球kau4

而ji4家gaa1掛kwaa3緊gan2三saam1號hou6波bo1，公gung1司si1照ziu3常soeng4開hoi1工gung1。
现在挂着三号风球，公司照常上班。
Signal No. 3 is up, and the office is open as usual.

除ceoi4波bo1 —— 除ceoi4下haa6風fung1球kau4

八baat3號hou6波bo1除ceoi4波bo1之zi1後hau6兩loeng5個go3鐘zung1，大daai6家gaa1要jiu3返faan1公gung1司si1。
八号风球除下后两个小时，大家要回公司。
Everyone has to be back two hours after Signal No. 8 is lowered.

喺hai2屋uk1企kei2做zou6嘢je5 —— 在zoi6家gaa1工gung1作zok3

車ce1唔m4多do1，老lou5細sai3話waa6今gam1日jat6喺hai2屋uk1企kei2做zou6嘢je5。
车不多，老板说今天在家工作。
There isn't much transport running, so the boss says we work from home today.

等dang2通tung1知zi1 —— 等dang2候hau6通tung1知zi1

風fung1好hou2大daai6，你nei5哋dei6先sin1唔m4好hou2出ceot1門mun4，等dang2通tung1知zi1。
风很大，你们先别出门，等通知。
The wind is strong, don't leave home yet, wait for notice.
