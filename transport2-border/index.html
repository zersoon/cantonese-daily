過gwo3關gwaan1北bak1上soeng5：回wui4鄉hoeng1證zing3、高gou1鐵tit3、排paai4隊deoi2

Crossing the Border: Home Return Permit, High-Speed Rail, Queuing

近gan6年nin4好hou2多do1香hoeng1港gong2人jan4週zau1末mut6「北bak1上soeng5」，坐zo6高gou1鐵tit3或waak6者ze2過gwo3關gwaan1去heoi3深sam1圳zan3。講gong2過gwo3關gwaan1嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

In recent years many Hong Kongers head north on weekends, taking the high-speed rail or crossing the border to Shenzhen. Border-crossing Cantonese, with Jyutping.

過gwo3關gwaan1 —— 过gwo3海hoi2关gwaan1

星sing1期kei4六luk6過gwo3關gwaan1嘅ge3人jan4好hou2多do1。
星期六过关的人很多。
Lots of people cross the border on Saturdays.

回wui4鄉hoeng1證zing3 —— 港gong2澳ou3居geoi1民man4来loi4往wong5内noi6地dei6通tung1行hang4证zing3

記gei3得dak1帶daai3回wui4鄉hoeng1證zing3。
记得带回乡证。
Remember to bring your Home Return Permit.

高gou1鐵tit3 —— 高gou1铁tit3

坐co5高gou1鐵tit3上soeng5深sam1圳zan3，十sap6幾gei2分fan1鐘zung1就zau6到dou3。
坐高铁去深圳，十几分钟就到。
Take the high-speed rail to Shenzhen and you're there in just over ten minutes.

排paai4隊deoi2 —— 排paai4队deoi2

關gwaan1口hau2排paai4隊deoi2排paai4咗zo2半bun3個go3鐘zung1。
在口岸排队排了半个小时。
We queued at the checkpoint for half an hour.

北bak1上soeng5 —— 去heoi3内noi6地dei6

好hou2多do1香hoeng1港gong2人jan4週zau1末mut6北bak1上soeng5食sik6飯faan6。
很多香港人周末去内地吃饭。
Many Hong Kongers head north for meals on weekends.
