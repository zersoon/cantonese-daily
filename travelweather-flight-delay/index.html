Flight Delays in Bad Weather: Delayed, Rebooking, the Gate, a Night at the Airport

天tin1氣hei3唔m4好hou2，飛fei1機gei1起hei2唔m4到dou2飛fei1，成seng4班baan1人jan4困kwan3喺hai2機gei1場coeng4。學hok6識sik1講gong2延jin4誤ng6、改goi2簽cim1同tung4轉zyun3閘zaap6口hau2，出ceot1門mun4旅leoi5行hang4唔m4使sai2手sau2忙mong4腳goek3亂lyun6。附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

When the weather turns bad, planes can't take off and everyone is stuck at the airport. Learn 'delayed', 'rebook' and 'gate change'. With Jyutping.

交通天氣進階

延jin4誤ng6 —— 延jin4误ng6

因jan1為wai6天tin1氣hei3唔m4好hou2，我ngo5班baan1機gei1延jin4誤ng6咗zo2三saam1個go3鐘zung1。
因为天气不好，我的航班延误了三个小时。
Because of the bad weather, my flight is delayed by three hours.

改goi2簽cim1 —— 改goi2签cim1

趕gon2唔m4切cit3轉zyun3機gei1，要jiu3去heoi3櫃gwai6位wai2改goi2簽cim1。
赶不上转机，要去柜台改签。
I won't make my connection, so I have to rebook at the counter.

轉zyun3閘zaap6口hau2 —— 换wun6登dang1机gei1口hau2

廣gwong2播bo3話waa6轉zyun3咗zo2閘zaap6口hau2，我ngo5哋dei6要jiu3行haang4返faan1過gwo3去heoi3。
广播说换了登机口，我们要走回去。
The announcement said the gate has changed, we need to walk back over.

起hei2唔m4到dou2飛fei1 —— 起hei2飞fei1不bat1了liu5

外ngoi6面min6行haang4雷leoi4，飛fei1機gei1暫zaam6時si4起hei2唔m4到dou2飛fei1。
外面打雷，飞机暂时起飞不了。
There's thunder outside, the plane can't take off for now.

喺hai2機gei1場coeng4過gwo3夜je6 —— 在zoi6机gei1场coeng4过gwo3夜je2

航hong4班baan1取ceoi2消siu1咗zo2，今gam1晚maan5要jiu3喺hai2機gei1場coeng4過gwo3夜je6。
航班取消了，今晚要在机场过夜。
The flight was cancelled, we'll have to spend the night at the airport.
