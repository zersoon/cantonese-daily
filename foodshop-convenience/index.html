便bin6利lei6店dim3買maai5嘢je5：叮ding1熱jit6、順seon6手sau2、拍paak3卡kaat1

At the Convenience Store: Microwaving, Grabbing Something on the Way, Tapping to Pay

香hoeng1港gong2幾gei2步bou6就zau6有jau5間gaan1便bin6利lei6店dim3，買maai5個go3飯faan6盒hap2叫giu3店dim3員jyun4「叮ding1」一jat1叮ding1，拍paak3卡kaat1就zau6走zau2。便bin6利lei6店dim3最zeoi3常soeng4聽teng1到dou3嘅ge3說syut3話waa6，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Hong Kong has a convenience store every few steps. Get a boxed meal microwaved, tap your card and go. What you hear most at the convenience store, with Jyutping.

飲食購物俚語

叮ding1 —— 用jung6微mei4波bo1爐lou4加gaa1熱jit6

個go3飯faan6盒hap2要jiu3唔m4要jiu3叮ding1？
这个盒饭要不要微波加热？
Do you want the boxed meal microwaved?

叮ding1一jat1分fan1鐘zung1就zau6得dak1。
微波一分钟就可以了。
One minute in the microwave will do.

順seon6手sau2 —— 順seon6便bin2

你nei5落lok6街gaai1順seon6手sau2幫bong1我ngo5買maai5支zi1水seoi2。
你下楼顺便帮我买瓶水。
Grab me a bottle of water while you're downstairs.

拍paak3卡kaat1 —— 用jung6卡kaa1嘟dou1一jat1下haa5俾bei2錢cin2

八baat3達daat6通tung1拍paak3卡kaat1就zau6得dak1。
用八达通刷一下就行。
Just tap your Octopus card.

飲jam2凍dung3嘢je5 —— 喝hot3冷laang5飲jam2

熱jit6到dou3死sei2，入jap6去heoi3買maai5啲di1凍dung3嘢je5飲jam2。
热死了，进去买点冷饮喝。
It's boiling hot. Let's go in and get something cold to drink.
