潮ciu4濕sap1定ding6乾gon1燥cou3：回wui4南naam4天tin1、焗guk6、抽cau1濕sap1機gei1

Humid or Dry: Damp Spring Days, Muggy Weather, Dehumidifiers

香hoeng1港gong2春ceon1天tin1「回wui4南naam4」好hou2潮ciu4濕sap1，秋cau1天tin1又jau6好hou2乾gon1燥cou3。講gong2濕sap1度dou6嘅ge3廣gwong2東dung1話waa2，附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3同tung4例lai6句geoi3。

Hong Kong springs are damp and sticky, autumns are dry. Cantonese for talking about humidity, with Jyutping and examples.

天氣進階香港

潮ciu4濕sap1 —— 潮ciu4湿sap1

春ceon1天tin1好hou2潮ciu4濕sap1，啲di1衫saam1晾long6極gik6都dou1唔m4乾gon1。
春天很潮湿，衣服怎么晾都不干。
Spring is so humid, the laundry never dries.

回wui4南naam4天tin1 —— 返faan2潮ciu4的dik1天tin1气hei3

回wui4南naam4天tin1，地dei6下haa2濕sap1晒saai3。
回南天，地板全湿了。
It's one of those damp spring days, the floor is all wet.

焗guk6 —— 闷mun6热jit6

今gam1日jat6好hou2焗guk6，成seng4身san1都dou1係hai6汗hon6。
今天很闷热，满身是汗。
It's so muggy today, I'm covered in sweat.

抽cau1濕sap1機gei1 —— 除ceoi4湿sap1机gei1

開hoi1咗zo2成seng4晚maan5抽cau1濕sap1機gei1，水seoi2箱soeng1滿mun5晒saai3。
开了一整晚除湿机，水箱全满了。
The dehumidifier ran all night and the tank is full.

乾gon1燥cou3 —— 干gon1燥cou3

秋cau1天tin1天tin1氣hei3乾gon1燥cou3，嘴zeoi2唇seon4好hou2易ji6爆baau3。
秋天天气干燥，嘴唇很容易干裂。
Autumn is dry, your lips crack easily.
