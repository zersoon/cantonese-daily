Comparing Prices: Pricier Than, About the Same, Shop Around, Worth It?

買maai5大daai6件gin6嘢je5之zi1前cin4梗gang2係hai6要jiu3格gaak3價gaa3！學hok6講gong2貴gwai3過gwo3、差caa1唔m4多do1、睇tai2多do1幾gei2間gaan1同tung4值zik6唔m4值zik6。

Before a big purchase you should compare prices. Learn to say 'pricier than', 'about the same', 'shop around' and 'is it worth it'.

購物語法日常會話

格gaak3價gaa3 —— 比bei2价gaai3

買maai5之zi1前cin4去heoi3幾gei2間gaan1舖pou3格gaak3下haa5價gaa3先sin1。
买之前先去几家店比比价。
Compare prices at a few shops before buying.

貴gwai3過gwo3 —— 比bei2……贵gwai3

呢ni1間gaan1貴gwai3過gwo3隔gaak3籬lei4五ng5十sap6蚊man1。
这家比隔壁贵五十块。
This shop is 50 dollars dearer than the one next door.

差caa1唔m4多do1 —— 差caa1不bat1多do1

兩loeng5間gaan1價gaa3錢cin4差caa1唔m4多do1。
两家价钱差不多。
The two shops' prices are about the same.

睇tai2多do1幾gei2間gaan1 —— 多do1看hon3几gei1家gaa1

唔m4使sai2急gap1，睇tai2多do1幾gei2間gaan1先sin1決kyut3定ding6。
不用急，多看几家再决定。
No rush, look at a few more shops before deciding.

值zik6唔m4值zik6 —— 值zik6不bat1值zik6

貴gwai3咗zo2一jat1千cin1蚊man1，你nei5覺gok3得dak1值zik6唔m4值zik6？
贵了一千块，你觉得值不值？
It's a thousand dollars more. Do you think it's worth it?
