Hailing a Cab in the Rain: Flagging a Taxi, No Umbrella, Soaked, Long Queues

一jat1落lok6大daai6雨jyu5，的dik1士si2就zau6特dak6別bit6難naan4截zit6。學hok6識sik1講gong2截zit6的dik1士si2、冇mou5帶daai3遮ze1同tung4濕sap1晒saai3，落lok6雨jyu5天tin1出ceot1街gaai1都dou1識sik1表biu2達daat6。附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

The moment it pours, taxis get hard to find. Learn how to say 'hail a taxi', 'no umbrella' and 'soaked through'. With Jyutping.

交通天氣香港

截zit6的dik1士si2 —— 拦laan4出ceot1租zou1车ce1

落lok6雨jyu5天tin1好hou2難naan4截zit6的dik1士si2，等dang2咗zo2半bun3個go3鐘zung1都dou1未mei6有jau5。
下雨天很难拦出租车，等了半个小时都还没有。
It's hard to get a taxi on a rainy day, I've waited half an hour and still nothing.

冇mou5帶daai3遮ze1 —— 没mut6带daai3伞saan3

我ngo5冇mou5帶daai3遮ze1，可ho2唔m4可ho2以ji5同tung4你nei5遮ze1埋maai4一jat1份fan6？
我没带伞，可以跟你一起撑吗？
I didn't bring an umbrella, can I share yours?

濕sap1晒saai3 —— 全cyun4湿sap1了liu5

行haang4咗zo2兩loeng5步bou6，對deoi3鞋haai4已ji5經ging1濕sap1晒saai3。
走了两步，鞋子已经全湿了。
After just a few steps, my shoes are completely soaked.

排paai4長coeng4隊deoi2 —— 排paai4长zoeng2队deoi6

的dik1士si2站zaam6排paai4長coeng4隊deoi2，不bat1如jyu4行haang4去heoi3港gong2鐵tit3站zaam6啦laa1。
出租车站排着长队，不如走去港铁站吧。
There's a long queue at the taxi stand, let's just walk to the MTR station.
