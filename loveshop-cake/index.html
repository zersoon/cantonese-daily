網mong5上soeng6訂ding3蛋daan6糕gou1：寫se2字zi6、蠟laap6燭zuk1、幾gei2磅bong6、自zi6取ceoi2

Ordering a Cake Online: Writing on It, Candles, How Many Pounds, Self Pick-up

幫bong1另ling6一jat1半bun3慶hing3祝zuk1生saang1日jat6，網mong5上soeng6訂ding3蛋daan6糕gou1要jiu3講gong2清cing1楚co2寫se2咩me1字zi6、要jiu3幾gei2磅bong6、要jiu3唔m4要jiu3蠟laap6燭zuk1，同tung4埋maai4自zi6取ceoi2定ding6送sung3貨fo3。附fu6粵jyut6拼ping3。

Celebrating your partner's birthday? When ordering a cake online, specify the message, the size in pounds, candles, and pick-up or delivery. With Jyutping.

訂deng6蛋daan6糕gou1 —— 订ding3蛋daan6糕gou1

我ngo5喺hai2網mong5上soeng6幫bong1佢keoi5訂deng6咗zo2個go3生saang1日jat6蛋daan6糕gou1。
我在网上给她订了个生日蛋糕。
I ordered her a birthday cake online.

幾gei2磅bong6 —— 几gei1磅bong6

兩loeng5個go3人jan4食sik6，一jat1磅bong6就zau6夠gau3。
两个人吃，一磅就够了。
For two people, one pound is enough.

寫se2字zi6 —— 写se2字zi6

蛋daan6糕gou1面min2可ho2唔m4可ho2以ji5寫se2字zi6？
蛋糕上面可以写字吗？
Can you write something on top of the cake?

蠟laap6燭zuk1 —— 蜡zaa3烛zuk1

記gei3得dak1要jiu3蠟laap6燭zuk1同tung4刀dou1叉caa1。
记得要蜡烛和刀叉。
Remember to ask for candles and cutlery.

自zi6取ceoi2 —— 自zi6取ceoi2

自zi6取ceoi2有jau5折zit3，我ngo5放fong3工gung1順seon6路lou6去heoi3拎ling1。
自取有折扣，我下班顺路去拿。
Self pick-up has a discount, so I'll grab it on my way home from work.
